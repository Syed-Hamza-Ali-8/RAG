# Tax Advisory Services Agreement — Sunridge Schools Trust

## 1. Parties and Background

### 1.1 Parties

This Tax Advisory Services Agreement ("Agreement") is between Globex Partners LLP ("Globex"), an accounting firm headquartered in Lahore, and Sunridge Schools Trust ("Sunridge" or "the Client"), a trust operating 22 private schools in Lahore, Islamabad and Faisalabad.

### 1.2 Background

Sunridge collects tuition and admission fees from about 18,000 students, employs about 1,400 staff and pays a large number of suppliers. It wants a single adviser to manage its tax compliance, review withholding tax obligations and assess the tax treatment of its fee structure.

### 1.3 Signature

This Agreement was signed on 25 August 2025 by Omar Sheikh, partner and head of Tax Advisory, for Globex, and by Farhan Qadir, Chief Financial Officer, for Sunridge. Sunridge's board approved the appointment on 18 August 2025.

## 2. Definitions

- "Annual Fee" means the fee in clause 6.1.
- "Deliverables" means the returns, statements, reports and advice listed in Schedule A.
- "Client Data" means all information provided by Sunridge to Globex, including financial records, payroll data and supplier records.
- "Ledger Vault" means Globex's secure client document portal.
- "Tax Authority" means the Federal Board of Revenue and any provincial revenue authority relevant to Sunridge.

## 3. Services

### 3.1 Tax Compliance

Globex will prepare and file Sunridge's annual income tax return and supporting statements, and prepare the annual withholding tax statements for salaries and supplier payments, based on information provided by Sunridge.

### 3.2 Withholding Tax Advisory

Globex will review Sunridge's withholding tax processes every quarter, test a sample of supplier and salary payments, and report errors with recommended corrections.

### 3.3 Fee Structure Tax Health Check

Globex will review the tax treatment of Sunridge's tuition, admission and transport fees and its scholarship scheme, and deliver a written report with recommendations by 31 March 2026.

### 3.4 Tax Authority Correspondence

Globex will respond on Sunridge's behalf to routine notices from the Tax Authority about filings Globex prepared. Representation in audits, appeals or litigation is outside this Agreement and requires a separate engagement.

### 3.5 Exclusions

This Agreement does not include statutory audit, bookkeeping, payroll processing, or advice on matters outside Pakistan.

## 4. Term and Termination

### 4.1 Term

This Agreement runs from 1 September 2025 to 31 December 2026.

### 4.2 Termination by Notice

Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason by giving 60 days' written notice. Fees for work performed up to the termination date remain payable.

### 4.3 Termination for Breach

Either party may terminate immediately by notice if the other commits a material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice.

### 4.4 Renewal

The Agreement does not renew automatically. The parties intend to discuss a renewal from October 2026. If no renewal is signed by 31 December 2026, the Agreement ends and Globex will return or delete Client Data as described in clause 9.5.

## 8. Deliverable Standards

## 9. Information and Data Protection

### 9.1 Information Exchange

All documents are exchanged through Ledger Vault. Sunridge users log in with an email one-time passcode. Files must not be sent by email.

### 9.2 Data Location

Sunridge's files are stored in Ledger Vault's primary storage in Tessera Cloud's Bahrain region, with encrypted backups in Tessera's Frankfurt region. Ledger Vault is hosted by Tessera Cloud Ltd under Globex's agreement TC-7720.

### 9.3 Payroll Data

Payroll extracts contain staff personal data. Globex will limit access to payroll files to the withholding tax specialist and the engagement manager, and will delete payroll extracts within 90 days after the related withholding statement is filed.

### 9.4 Security Incidents

Globex will notify Sunridge of any security incident affecting Client Data within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

### 9.5 Return or Deletion at the End of the Agreement

When the Agreement ends, Globex will, at Sunridge's choice, return Client Data through Ledger Vault or delete it within 30 days, except for working papers that Globex must keep under professional rules. Globex will confirm return or deletion in writing. This record is also needed for Globex's ISO 27001 surveillance audit in March 2027.

## 10. Confidentiality

Globex will keep all Client Data confidential and use it only to provide the services. These obligations continue for five years after the Agreement ends.

## 11. Liability

Globex's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the Annual Fee. Globex is not liable for penalties or default surcharges caused by late or inaccurate information from Sunridge, or for indirect loss.

## 12. Conflicts of Interest

Globex has checked that it has no conflict of interest in acting for Sunridge. Globex does not audit Sunridge. If Sunridge later asks Globex to become its auditor, Globex will review whether it can continue to provide tax services under independence rules.

## 13. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Pakistan. Disputes not resolved by the engagement partner and Sunridge's CFO within 30 days will be referred to arbitration in Lahore under the Arbitration Act 1940, by a sole arbitrator.

## Schedule F — Withholding Tax Review Method

### F.1 Sampling

Each quarter Globex selects a sample of 60 supplier payments and 40 salary payments from the quarter's ledger extract, weighted towards high-value payments, new suppliers and payments made by individual schools rather than head office.

### F.2 Tests

For each sampled payment Globex checks that the correct withholding rate was applied for the type of payment and the supplier's tax status; that tax was deducted at the time of payment; that it was deposited with the Tax Authority by the due date; and that the supplier received a certificate of deduction.

### F.3 Reporting

Each quarterly report rates findings as high, medium or low. High findings, such as tax not deducted on a payment above PKR 1 million, are reported to the CFO within two working days of being found rather than waiting for the quarterly report.

## Schedule L — Fee Structure Health Check: Detailed Findings

### L.1 Transport Fees

Several schools invoiced transport fees as part of a single "tuition and services" line. Because transport services may be treated differently from tuition for sales tax on services, combining them created a risk of under-declaring tax on the transport element. Recommendation: invoice transport fees as a separate line from the 2026–27 academic year. Status: implemented for invoices issued from August 2026.

### L.2 Scholarships

Scholarships were granted by school principals without written criteria. Without documented criteria, the tax treatment of scholarship discounts could be challenged. Recommendation: adopt a board-approved scholarship policy with eligibility criteria and record each award. Status: policy approved by the board in April 2026.

### L.3 Fees Received in Advance

Sunridge collects the first term's fees for the next academic year in May and June. The report recommended reviewing the timing of income recognition and the related tax treatment with Sunridge's auditors. Status: discussed with the auditors in July 2026; no change needed for tax year 2026.

### L.4 Supplier Withholding at Schools

Each school paid local suppliers directly and applied withholding inconsistently. Recommendation: centralise supplier payments and withholding in head office. Status: implemented from 1 May 2026, which reduced withholding errors in the Q2 review from 9 to 5.

Ledger Vault was unavailable for 6 hours 10 minutes on 30 March 2026, three days after the fee structure health check report was delivered on 27 March.
