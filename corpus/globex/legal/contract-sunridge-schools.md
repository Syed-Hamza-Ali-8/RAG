# Tax Advisory Services Contract — Sunridge Schools Trust

## Parties

Contract between Globex Partners LLP and Sunridge Schools Trust, a network of private schools in Pakistan.

## Services

Annual tax compliance, withholding tax advisory and a tax health check of the Trust's fee structure.

## Term

From 1 September 2025 to 31 December 2026. Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice.

## Fees

PKR 14.4 million per year, billed quarterly at PKR 3.6 million.

## Signature

Signed by Omar Sheikh, Tax partner, for Globex.
