# Audit Engagement Letter — Marlowe Retail Group plc

## Parties

Engagement letter between Globex Partners LLP and Marlowe Retail Group plc, a UK retailer, for the statutory audit of the financial year ending 31 December 2026.

## Signature

Signed on 2 March 2026 by Imran Siddiqui, Managing Partner, for Globex, and by Hannah Price, CFO, for Marlowe Retail Group.

## Audit Fee

The audit fee is GBP 285,000, billed as an interim fee of GBP 95,000 in August 2026 and GBP 190,000 on delivery of the audit report.

## Timetable

Interim fieldwork: August 2026. Final fieldwork: February and March 2027. The audit report is to be delivered by 30 April 2027.

## Data Handling

Marlowe's files are exchanged through Ledger Vault and stored in the London region only.

## Term

The engagement letter covers the FY2026 audit only. Reappointment for FY2027 is subject to shareholder approval.
