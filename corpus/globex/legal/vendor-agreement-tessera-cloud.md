# Cloud Services Agreement — Tessera Cloud

## Parties

Agreement TC-7720 between Tessera Cloud Ltd and Globex Partners LLP.

## Signature

Signed on 1 April 2024 by Laura Bennett, CFO, for Globex.

## Term

Three years, from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2027. The agreement renews automatically for one year unless either party gives 60 days' notice.

## Fees

USD 15,000 per month.

## Service Level Agreement

Tessera commits to 99.95% monthly availability. Service credits:

- Below 99.95%: 5% of the monthly fee.
- Below 99.5%: 15% of the monthly fee.

Credits must be claimed within 15 days of the incident.

## Data Location

Data stays in the region where Globex stores it.
