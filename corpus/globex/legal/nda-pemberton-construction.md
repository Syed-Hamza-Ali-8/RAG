# Non-Disclosure Agreement — Pemberton Construction Ltd

## Parties

A mutual NDA between Globex Partners LLP and Pemberton Construction Ltd, a UAE construction company, covering a potential forensic accounting engagement.

## Term

Signed on 20 January 2026 by Henrik Dahl, Legal Counsel. The NDA lasts three years and expires on 19 January 2029.

## Governing Law

Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) law.
