# ISO 27001 Surveillance Audit Plan 2027

## Audit Details

The 2027 ISO 27001 surveillance audit will be carried out by Albion Assurance Certification from 8 to 10 March 2027, under contract AAC-2027-14. Lead on Globex's side: Henrik Dahl, Legal Counsel. Technical lead: Kwame Mensah, Head of IT. Globex has held ISO 27001 certification since 2021; this is the second surveillance audit of the current three-year certification cycle.

The audit falls in busy season (15 January to 31 March). To limit disruption, interviews with audit staff will be held in the Lahore office on 9 March only, and no more than five audit staff will be interviewed.

## Audit Fee

Albion's fee is USD 9,500. The 2026 budget includes USD 10,000 for the surveillance audit.

## Scope

The audit covers Ledger Vault, Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, the Fernbook integration, and the Lahore and Manchester offices. The Abu Dhabi office is sampled remotely.

## Preconditions

- Every vendor processing client data must have an agreement in force on the audit dates.
- Every expired client contract must have a documented data return or deletion record.
- Incident records for the year must show root cause analysis and completed actions.
- The busy-season capacity review and the latest restore tests must be documented.

If the Sunridge agreement is not renewed, Globex must return or delete Sunridge's data within 30 days after 31 December 2026 and confirm it in writing. Albion will check that record. A renewal proposal is with Sunridge's board for November 2026.

The Tessera agreement is in force on the audit dates whatever the renewal decision, but Albion will ask how the renewal is being managed given the March 2026 outage. The renewal decision memo, the exit plan and the outage postmortem will be in the evidence pack.

Fernbook's licence ends on 31 December 2026. Because Fernbook holds audit working papers that reference client data, an in-force agreement on the audit dates is required.

## Focus Areas Requested by Albion

At the 2026 surveillance audit, Albion raised two minor nonconformities:

1. Supplier monitoring: no evidence that Tessera's availability was reviewed against the SLA each month.
2. Capacity management: no documented capacity planning for Ledger Vault.

Both are now addressed: finance reviews Tessera's monthly usage and availability reports, and the IT team runs a capacity review every December.

## Lessons From the 2026 Surveillance Audit

Albion's 2026 visit found that Globex's incident records did not consistently record root causes, and that capacity planning for Ledger Vault was informal. The 30 March 2026 Ledger Vault outage, which happened a few weeks after that visit, confirmed the capacity finding. Both postmortems written in 2026 now follow a template with root cause, contributing factors, action items and owners, and the capacity review is scheduled every December. Albion will sample both postmortems in March 2027.

Albion's auditor will spend 8 March 2027 in the Lahore office with the IT team and the Legal Counsel, 9 March interviewing up to five audit staff, and 10 March remotely with the Manchester office, followed by the closing meeting.
