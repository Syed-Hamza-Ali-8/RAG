# Globex Service Catalog

## Statutory Audit

Annual statutory audits under ISA and local standards. Fees are agreed in an engagement letter before fieldwork starts. Audit fieldwork for December year-ends runs from January to March, which the firm calls busy season.

## Tax Advisory

Corporate tax returns, transfer pricing documentation and tax health checks. Ongoing tax advisory is contracted annually with quarterly billing.

## Forensic Accounting

Fraud investigations, dispute support and expert witness services, billed on time and materials.

## Ledger Vault Client Portal

Every client receives access to Ledger Vault, Globex's secure document exchange portal, used to upload trial balances, contracts and audit evidence.

## How to Engage Us

Contact the client acceptance team. Every new client passes anti-money-laundering and independence checks before an engagement letter is issued.
