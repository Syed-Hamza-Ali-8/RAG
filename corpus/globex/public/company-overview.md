# Globex Partners LLP — Company Overview

## Who We Are

Globex Partners LLP is an accounting and audit firm founded in 2006 in Lahore by Imran Siddiqui, who remains Managing Partner. The firm has around 310 staff in three offices: Lahore (headquarters), Abu Dhabi and Manchester.

## Service Lines

- Audit and Assurance, led by partner Nadia Hussain.
- Tax Advisory, led by partner Omar Sheikh.
- Forensic Accounting, led by partner Claire Whitfield.

## Clients

Globex audits and advises retailers, school groups, construction companies and family-owned businesses in Pakistan, the UAE and the United Kingdom.

## Certifications

Globex has held ISO 27001 certification since 2021. The next surveillance audit is scheduled for March 2027.
