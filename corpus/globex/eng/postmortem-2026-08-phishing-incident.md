# Postmortem: Phishing Incident, 19 August 2026

## Summary

On 19 August 2026, three staff mailboxes were compromised after a phishing email led to repeated MFA push requests that the users eventually approved ("MFA fatigue"). Severity: Sev2. Incident commander: Kwame Mensah.

## Impact

The attacker sent internal phishing emails from the compromised mailboxes for about 40 minutes. No client files in Ledger Vault were accessed; Ledger Vault requires a separate login.

## Action Items

1. Enable MFA number matching for all staff. Owner: IT team. Done 22 August 2026.
2. Run phishing awareness training for all staff by 30 September 2026. Owner: HR and IT.
3. Notify the Legal Counsel of every suspected mailbox compromise within one hour.
