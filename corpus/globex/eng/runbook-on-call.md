# Runbook: On-Call Rotation

## Rotation

The IT on-call rotation changes every Wednesday at 09:00 PKT.

## Response Times

- Sev1: acknowledge within 20 minutes.
- Sev2: acknowledge within 1 hour.

## Escalation

Unresolved Sev1 incidents are escalated after 45 minutes to Kwame Mensah, Head of IT.

## Allowance

The on-call allowance is PKR 12,000 per week.
