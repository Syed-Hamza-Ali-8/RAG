# Procurement Policy

## Quotes

Purchases above USD 5,000 need three quotes.

## Approval

Contracts above USD 25,000 per year need sign-off by a partner and the CFO.

## Data Protection

Any vendor handling client data needs approval from the Head of IT and the Legal Counsel and must sign a data processing agreement.

## Renewals

Critical vendor contracts are reviewed 90 days before they end.
