# Procurement Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy makes sure Globex buys goods and services at fair prices from vendors that protect client data and do not threaten the firm's independence. It applies to all offices and all purchases. It is owned by Laura Bennett, CFO.

## Purchase Requests

Purchases above USD 1,000 start with a purchase request approved by the budget holder. Smaller purchases may be made on a firm card and claimed in Claimly.

## Quotes

Purchases above USD 5,000 need three quotes. Single-source purchases must be justified in writing and approved by the CFO.

## Approval

Contracts above USD 25,000 per year need sign-off by a partner and the CFO. Contracts below that amount are approved by the budget holder. All contracts are reviewed by the Legal Counsel, Henrik Dahl, before signature.

## Independence Checks

Because Globex is an audit firm, it must not enter business relationships with audit clients that could threaten independence. Before signing any contract, the finance team checks the vendor against the list of audit clients. A purchase from an audit client is allowed only if it is on normal commercial terms and immaterial to both parties, and must be approved by the Ethics Partner.

## Data Protection

Any vendor handling client data needs approval from the Head of IT and the Legal Counsel and must sign a data processing agreement. The agreement must include:

- security incident notification within 12 hours where possible, and never more than 24 hours, so Globex can meet the 72-hour client notification commitments in its engagement letters;
- data location commitments that match client requirements, such as keeping UK client files in the UK;
- deletion of data at the end of the contract;
- access to independent certifications such as ISO 27001.

Tessera Cloud's agreement TC-7720 meets these requirements with a 12-hour notification commitment.

## Service Levels

Contracts for services that client work depends on must include a measurable service level, service credits and a claim process. Finance records each claim window in the vendor register, because some windows are short; Tessera's is 15 days from the incident.

## Critical Vendors

A vendor is critical if client work depends on it. Current critical vendors are Tessera Cloud (Ledger Vault hosting) and Fernbook (audit working papers). Critical vendors need a named business owner, an exit plan, and a renewal review at least 90 days before the contract ends.

## Renewals

Automatic renewals must be tracked so that notice can be given in time. For example, Tessera's agreement renews automatically for one year on 31 March 2027 unless notice is given 60 days before, by 30 January 2027. Finance adds every notice deadline to the vendor register and reminds the business owner 30 days before it.

## Paying Vendors

Vendor invoices are approved in Claimly's accounts payable module after checking them against the purchase request or contract. Invoices from critical vendors are also checked against usage reports, and any Service Credit due is tracked until it appears on an invoice.

## Exceptions

Exceptions require written approval from the CFO and a partner, recorded with the purchase request.

## Vendor Register

Every signed contract is added to the vendor register within five working days, with its reference, business owner, annual value, end date, notice deadline, Service Credit claim window and criticality. The register is reviewed quarterly by the CFO and is used as evidence in Globex's ISO 27001 audits.

## Exit Plans

Exit plans for critical vendors describe how data would be moved to another provider, how long it would take, and which clients would need to be told. For Tessera Cloud, the plan keeps UK client files in the UK throughout any migration, as required by engagement letters with UK clients such as Marlowe Retail Group.

## Training

Budget holders complete a short procurement and independence training module when they are given budget responsibility, and again every two years.

## Example: Replacing the Hosting Provider for Ledger Vault

If Globex decides not to renew Tessera Cloud, the replacement provider would be a critical vendor handling all client files. The purchase needs three quotes, sign-off by a partner and the CFO, a data processing agreement with incident notification within 12 hours, a commitment to keep UK client files in the UK, and an independence check against the audit client list. Notice to Tessera must be given by 30 January 2027, and the migration should be planned for April to June 2027, after busy season and after the ISO 27001 surveillance audit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need three quotes for a USD 4,000 purchase?** No; three quotes are needed above USD 5,000.

**Can we buy software from an audit client?** Only on normal commercial terms, if immaterial to both sides, and with Ethics Partner approval.

**Who reviews data processing agreements?** The Legal Counsel, Henrik Dahl, together with the Head of IT.
