# Expense Policy

## Submitting Claims

Claims are submitted in Claimly within 45 days, with receipts.

## Approval Limits

- Up to USD 300: line manager.
- USD 300 to USD 3,000: director or partner.
- Above USD 3,000: Chief Financial Officer (Laura Bennett).

## Travel

Economy class for all flights under eight hours. Per diems: USD 50 per day in Pakistan, USD 110 in the UAE, USD 100 in the UK.

## Client Recharges

Expenses recharged to clients must quote the engagement code.
