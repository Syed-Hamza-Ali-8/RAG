## Vendor Classification

A vendor is critical if client work depends on it. Critical vendors need a named business owner, an exit plan, a review of security certifications every year, and a renewal review at least 90 days before the contract ends, as required by the procurement policy. The Fernbook renewal review started on 2 October 2026, 90 days before its end date. The Tessera renewal review will start by 31 December 2026.
