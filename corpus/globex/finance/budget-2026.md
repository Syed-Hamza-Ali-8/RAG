# Budget 2026

## Approval

The 2026 budget was approved by the partners on 18 December 2025. Budget owner: Laura Bennett, CFO.

The revenue target for 2026 is USD 24 million: Audit and Assurance USD 13.5 million, Tax Advisory USD 7 million and Forensic Accounting USD 3.5 million.
