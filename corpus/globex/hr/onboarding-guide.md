# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

## Day One

New joiners receive a firm laptop, Microsoft 365 account, Ledger Vault access and Fernbook audit software training. Each joiner is assigned a counselling partner.

## Independence Declaration

All new joiners complete an independence declaration on day one, listing any financial interests and family relationships with clients.

## Probation

Probation lasts six months.

## Training

Mandatory training in the first month: information security, anti-money laundering and Fernbook fundamentals.
