# Performance Review Process

## Cycle

Globex holds one annual performance review in June, after busy season ends. Engagement managers provide feedback for every engagement an employee worked on during the year.

## Ratings

Ratings use four grades: A (exceptional), B (strong), C (developing) and D (below expectations).

## Moderation

Ratings are moderated by the partners' committee, chaired by the Managing Partner, Imran Siddiqui.

## Bonus

Employees rated A are eligible for a bonus of up to 20% of base salary; B up to 10%; C and D receive no performance bonus.

## Development Plans

Employees rated D receive a 90-day development plan agreed with their counselling partner.
