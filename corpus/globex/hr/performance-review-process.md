# Performance Review Process

## Purpose

The performance review process gives every employee feedback on their work across all engagements, links pay and promotion to performance, and supports professional development. It is owned by Aisha Farooq, HR Director.

## Cycle

Globex holds one annual performance review in June, after busy season ends. Ratings feed the July salary review, promotions and annual bonuses. There is also an informal mid-year conversation in December with the counselling partner, before busy season.

## Engagement Feedback

Because audit staff work on many engagements, engagement managers provide feedback for every engagement an employee worked on during the year. Feedback is recorded in the HR system within two weeks of the engagement's audit report being signed. Feedback covers technical quality, Fernbook documentation standards, client communication, teamwork and meeting deadlines.

## Self-Assessment

Each employee completes a self-assessment in May, covering their engagements, exam progress, training hours and development goals. Qualified staff confirm their continuing professional development hours for the year.

## Ratings

Ratings use four grades:

- A — exceptional performance across engagements.
- B — strong performance.
- C — developing; meets some but not all expectations for the grade.
- D — below expectations.

## Moderation

### Partners' Committee

Ratings proposed by counselling partners are moderated by the partners' committee, chaired by the Managing Partner, Imran Siddiqui. The committee compares ratings across service lines and offices so that the same standard applies in Lahore, Abu Dhabi and Manchester.

### Timing

Moderation takes place in the third week of June. Counselling partners must not share proposed ratings before moderation ends.

### Quality Findings

If an engagement an employee worked on received an adverse finding from the firm's internal quality review or an external inspection, the committee considers whether the employee's own work contributed, and documents its reasoning.

## Review Meeting

After moderation, each counselling partner holds a review meeting with each counsellee before 10 July. The employee receives their rating, written feedback and agreed objectives and training plan for the next year.

## Bonus

Employees rated A are eligible for a bonus of up to 20% of base salary; B up to 10%. C and D ratings receive no performance bonus. Exam bonuses (PKR 50,000 per ACCA or ICAP paper passed at the first attempt, in Lahore) are paid separately and do not depend on the rating.

## Promotion

Promotions are decided by the partners' committee at the same meeting, based on the rating, qualifications and readiness for the next grade. Promotion to Manager (G4) requires a professional qualification. Promotions take effect on 1 July.

## Development Plans

Employees rated D receive a 90-day development plan agreed with their counselling partner, with monthly check-ins and HR support. At the end of the plan, the outcome is successful completion, an extension of up to 60 days, or the formal capability procedure.

## Appeals

An employee may appeal their rating in writing to the HR Director within ten working days of the review meeting. A partner not involved in the original rating reviews the appeal with HR and responds within 20 working days. The appeal decision is final.

## 2026 Results

In the June 2026 review, 284 employees were rated: 41 A, 132 B, 98 C and 13 D. Thirteen development plans were started. Twenty-two employees were promoted, including six to Manager.

## Records

Review records are kept in the HR system for six years after an employee leaves and are visible only to the employee, their counselling partner, the partners' committee and HR.

## Feedback Quality

HR checks engagement feedback every quarter. Engagement managers with overdue feedback are reminded, and persistent gaps are raised with their own counselling partner, because missing feedback makes ratings less fair for staff who worked on many engagements.

## Calendar

- December: mid-year conversation with the counselling partner.
- May: self-assessments and final engagement feedback.
- Third week of June: partners' committee moderation.
- By 10 July: review meetings; promotions take effect on 1 July and salary changes in July payroll.

## Worked Example

A Senior Associate (G2) worked on six engagements in the year, including the Marlowe Retail Group audit and tax work for Sunridge Schools Trust. Engagement managers recorded feedback after each audit report was signed. The counselling partner proposes a B rating. At moderation in the third week of June, the partners' committee, chaired by Imran Siddiqui, compares the proposal with peers in Lahore and Manchester and confirms B. The employee is eligible for a bonus of up to 10% of base salary. Having passed two ACCA papers at the first attempt in the year, the employee also received PKR 100,000 in exam bonuses, which do not depend on the rating. With the qualification nearly complete, the committee considers the employee for promotion to Assistant Manager (G3) from 1 July.
