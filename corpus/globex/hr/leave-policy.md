# Leave Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Globex Partners LLP. Partners follow the partnership agreement.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per year. Up to 10 unused days may be carried over, and carried-over days must be used by 30 June.

### Busy Season Restriction

From 15 January to 31 March, annual leave of more than three consecutive days needs approval from the engagement partner.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Maternity leave is 18 weeks at full pay. Paternity leave is 2 weeks at full pay.

## Study Leave

Employees studying for ACCA, ICAEW or ICAP examinations receive 5 days of paid study leave per exam sitting.

## Requesting Leave

Leave is requested through the HR system and approved by the line manager.
