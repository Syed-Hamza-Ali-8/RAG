# Remote Work Policy

## Remote Days

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week. During busy season (15 January to 31 March) audit staff work from the office or the client site every day.

## Allowance

Lahore staff receive an internet allowance of PKR 6,000 per month.

## Security

Client files must only be accessed through Ledger Vault or Microsoft 365 on a firm-managed laptop. Working from outside the employee's home country requires approval from the HR Director.
