# Budget 2026

## Approval

The 2026 budget was approved by the board on 10 December 2025. Budget owner: Daniel Okafor, CFO.

## Revenue Target

The revenue target for 2026 is USD 18.5 million, split across practices as follows:

- Digital Transformation: USD 6.2 million
- Cloud Advisory (including Atlas subscriptions): USD 8.1 million
- Risk and Compliance: USD 4.2 million

## Major Cost Lines

| Line | 2026 budget (USD) |
|---|---|
| Cloud hosting (Cobalt Cloud) | 310,000 |
| Software subscriptions | 145,000 |
| Training and certifications | 120,000 |
| Travel | 450,000 |
| ISO 27001 recertification audit (Nordcert) | 26,000 |
| Recruitment | 210,000 |

## Controls

Spending above a cost line's budget needs CFO approval. Quarterly budget reviews take place in the first two weeks after each quarter ends.
