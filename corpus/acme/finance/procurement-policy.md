# Procurement Policy

## Quotes

Purchases above USD 10,000 require three written quotes, unless the vendor is the only supplier able to meet the requirement, which must be justified in writing.

## Security Review

Any vendor that will store or process client data needs a vendor security review by the platform team before the contract is signed. The review covers certifications, data location, encryption and incident history.

## Contract Signature

- Contracts below USD 50,000 per year: practice lead.
- Contracts of USD 50,000 per year or more: Chief Financial Officer.
- All contracts are reviewed by legal before signature.

## Critical Vendors

A vendor is critical if a client service depends on it. Critical vendors need a named business owner, an exit plan and a renewal review at least 120 days before the contract ends.
