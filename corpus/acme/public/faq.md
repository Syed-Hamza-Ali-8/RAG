# Employee FAQ

## What are standard office hours?

Core hours are 9:00 to 18:00 local time, Monday to Friday, in all three offices. Friday prayers are accommodated in Karachi and Dubai with a 12:30 to 14:00 break.

## How do I get IT help?

Raise a ticket in Beacon, the internal IT helpdesk portal. Urgent issues (you cannot work at all) can also be reported to the IT hotline extension 4357. Beacon tickets are answered within four business hours.

## How do I book business travel?

Travel is booked through the finance team using the travel request form in Ledgerline. Requests need line-manager approval before booking. See the Expense Policy for travel class rules.

## Where are company policies kept?

All policies are published on the intranet under "Policies". HR policies such as leave and remote work are also in PeopleDesk, the HR portal.

## Who do I contact about payroll?

Payroll questions go to the People team through PeopleDesk. Payslips are published on the 25th of each month.

## Can I use personal devices for work?

Only for email and chat through the approved mobile apps, and only with device encryption and a screen lock enabled. Client data must never be stored on personal devices.
