Acme Advisory Group expects every employee to act with integrity, respect and care for client confidentiality.

Gifts and hospitality: employees may accept gifts worth up to USD 50. Anything above USD 50 must be declared to the compliance team within five business days and may need to be returned.

Conflicts of interest: any personal or financial relationship with a client, supplier or competitor must be declared to your line manager and the General Counsel before you work on a related engagement.

Confidentiality: client information may only be shared with colleagues who need it for their work. Client data must stay within approved systems.

Speaking up: concerns can be raised confidentially with the General Counsel, Tomás Ferreira, or through the anonymous ethics line. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

Breaches of this code may lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.
