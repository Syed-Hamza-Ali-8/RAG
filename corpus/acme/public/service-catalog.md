# Acme Service Catalog

## Cloud Readiness Assessment

A four-week, fixed-fee engagement that reviews a client's applications, data and security posture and produces a migration roadmap. Delivered by the Cloud Advisory practice. Typical team: one manager and two consultants.

## Atlas Managed Analytics

Atlas is Acme's managed analytics platform. Clients receive dashboards, data pipelines and a monthly insights review. Atlas is billed as a monthly subscription with a 99.5% availability commitment. Onboarding a new Atlas client takes six to eight weeks.

## Cloud Migration Programmes

Multi-month programmes that move client workloads to the cloud. These are contracted under a Statement of Work (SOW) with milestone-based fees. Data residency requirements are agreed in the SOW before work starts.

## Compliance Advisory

Gap assessments and remediation support for ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II. A gap assessment takes three weeks. Remediation support is billed on a time-and-materials basis at the consultant day rates published to clients each January.

## Digital Operating Model Design

Workshops and design sprints that redesign how a client's teams work. Usually six to ten weeks.

## How to Engage Us

New enquiries go to the engagement desk. A partner responds within two business days. Every engagement starts with a signed Master Services Agreement or a Statement of Work.
