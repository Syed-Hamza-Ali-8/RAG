# Acme Advisory Group — Company Overview

## Who We Are

Acme Advisory Group is a management and technology consulting firm founded in 2011 in Karachi by Sara Qureshi and Daniel Okafor. Sara Qureshi serves as Chief Executive Officer and Daniel Okafor as Chief Financial Officer. The firm employs approximately 240 people across three offices: Karachi (headquarters), Dubai and London.

## Practices

Acme is organised into three practices:

- Digital Transformation, led by Farah Naqvi, which redesigns operating models and customer journeys.
- Cloud Advisory, led by Mei Lin Zhou, who is also the firm's Chief Technology Officer. This practice runs Atlas, our managed client analytics platform.
- Risk and Compliance, led by Tomás Ferreira, who is also General Counsel. This practice helps clients prepare for ISO 27001 and SOC 2.

## Clients and Sectors

We serve mid-sized organisations in logistics, healthcare and insurance. Our longest-running managed service is the Atlas analytics service for a European logistics client. Client names are confidential and are not published outside the firm.

## Certifications

Acme has held ISO 27001 certification since 2019. The next recertification audit is scheduled for February 2027.
