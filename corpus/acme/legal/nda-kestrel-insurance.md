# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — Kestrel Insurance Brokers

## Parties

A mutual non-disclosure agreement (NDA) between Acme Advisory Group and Kestrel Insurance Brokers, based in Dubai.

## Purpose

The parties are exploring a compliance advisory engagement covering ISO 27001 readiness for Kestrel's broking platform.

## Signature and Term

Signed on 10 June 2026 by Tomás Ferreira, General Counsel, for Acme. The NDA lasts two years and expires on 9 June 2028. Confidentiality obligations survive for three years after expiry.

## Governing Law

The NDA is governed by the laws of the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC).

## Permitted Disclosure

Confidential information may be shared only with employees and advisers who need to know it for the stated purpose and who are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.
