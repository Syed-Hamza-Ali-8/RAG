# Master Services Agreement — Halvorsen Logistics AS

## Parties

This Master Services Agreement (MSA) is between Acme Advisory Group ("Acme") and Halvorsen Logistics AS, a logistics company registered in Norway ("the Client").

## Signature

The MSA was signed on 1 February 2024 by Sara Qureshi, CEO, for Acme and by Erik Halvorsen, CEO, for the Client.

## Term and Renewal

The initial term is three years, from 1 February 2024 to 31 January 2027. The agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives at least 90 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the current term. For the initial term, the notice deadline is 2 November 2026.

## Services

Acme provides the Atlas managed analytics service, including data pipelines, dashboards and a monthly insights review.

## Fees

The monthly fee is EUR 38,000, invoiced monthly in advance. Invoices are payable within 30 days. Fees increase by the eurozone consumer price index on each renewal.

## Service Levels

Acme commits to 99.5% monthly availability for Atlas. If availability falls below 99.5% in a month, the Client receives a service credit of 5% of that month's fee; below 99.0%, the credit is 15%.

## Data Protection

Client data is processed in Cobalt Cloud's Frankfurt region, with disaster recovery in the Dubai region. A data processing addendum (DPA) forms part of this MSA. The DPA was last updated in March 2024.

## Liability

Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. The cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or data protection.

## Governing Law

This MSA is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
