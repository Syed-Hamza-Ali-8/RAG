# Master Services Agreement — Halvorsen Logistics AS

## 1. Parties and Background

### 1.1 Parties

This Master Services Agreement ("MSA" or "Agreement") is made between Acme Advisory Group, a consulting firm with its headquarters at Floor 7, Crescent Business Centre, Karachi ("Acme" or "the Supplier"), and Halvorsen Logistics AS, a freight and warehousing company registered in Norway with its head office in Bergen ("Halvorsen" or "the Client"). Acme and the Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

### 1.2 Background

The Client operates 14 distribution centres across Norway, Sweden and Denmark and wants a managed analytics service that consolidates warehouse, fleet and order data into a single set of operational dashboards. Acme operates Atlas, a managed analytics platform, and has agreed to provide the Atlas service to the Client on the terms of this Agreement.

### 1.3 Signature

This Agreement was signed on 1 February 2024 by Sara Qureshi, Chief Executive Officer, for Acme, and by Erik Halvorsen, Chief Executive Officer, for the Client. The Client's operational sponsor is Ingrid Solberg, Head of Data. Acme's engagement lead is Mei Lin Zhou, Chief Technology Officer, and the day-to-day service delivery manager is Bilal Ahmed.

## 2. Definitions

In this Agreement the following words have these meanings:

- "Atlas" means Acme's managed analytics platform, including its ingestion workers, data warehouse, dashboard service and supporting infrastructure.
- "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Norway or England.
- "Client Data" means all data supplied by or on behalf of the Client, or generated by Atlas from that data, including warehouse records, fleet telemetry and order histories.
- "Hosting Provider" means Cobalt Cloud GmbH, the cloud infrastructure provider used by Acme to host Atlas, or any replacement approved under clause 9.4.
- "Monthly Fee" means the recurring fee set out in clause 6.1, as adjusted under clause 6.3.
- "Service Credit" means the credit calculated under Schedule 2 when Atlas misses its availability commitment.
- "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a document agreed under clause 4 describing additional project work.
- "Subprocessor" means any third party that processes Client Data on Acme's behalf.

## 3. The Services

### 3.1 Atlas Managed Analytics

Acme will provide the Atlas managed analytics service described in Schedule 1. In summary, the service includes data pipelines from the Client's warehouse management system, transport management system and order platform; a modelled data warehouse refreshed every 15 minutes; operational dashboards for up to 250 named Client users; and a monthly insights review led by an Acme consultant.

### 3.2 Onboarding

Acme will onboard the Client to Atlas in an eight-week onboarding phase starting on the Effective Date. Onboarding includes connector set-up, data modelling, dashboard design workshops, user acceptance testing and training for up to 40 Client super-users. Onboarding is complete when the Client signs the onboarding acceptance certificate or uses Atlas in production for ten consecutive Business Days, whichever is earlier.

### 3.3 Service Hours and Support

Atlas is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, except during planned maintenance. The Acme service desk handles Client support requests from 07:00 to 19:00 Central European Time on Business Days. Severity 1 incidents (Atlas unavailable for all Client users) are handled 24 hours a day by Acme's platform on-call engineer.

### 3.4 Planned Maintenance

Planned maintenance takes place in a weekly window on Sundays between 01:00 and 05:00 Central European Time. Acme will give at least five Business Days' notice of any planned maintenance expected to make Atlas unavailable for more than 30 minutes. Planned maintenance does not count as downtime for the availability calculation.

## 4. Statements of Work

### 4.1 Additional Work

The Client may ask Acme to carry out project work in addition to the Atlas service, such as new data connectors, advanced forecasting models or integrations with the Client's finance systems. Each piece of additional work will be described in a Statement of Work signed by both Parties.

### 4.2 Content of a Statement of Work

Each SOW will state the scope, deliverables, timetable, fees, acceptance criteria and key personnel for the work. If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement prevails unless the SOW expressly states which clause it overrides.

### 4.3 Change Control

Either Party may request a change to the Atlas service or to an SOW by submitting a change request. Acme will respond within ten Business Days with an impact assessment covering cost, timetable and risk. No change is binding until both Parties sign it.

## 5. Term, Renewal and Termination

### 5.1 Initial Term

This Agreement starts on 1 February 2024 (the "Effective Date") and continues for an initial term of three years, ending on 31 January 2027.

### 5.2 Automatic Renewal

At the end of the initial term the Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal periods of 12 months each, unless either Party gives the other at least 90 days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the current term. For the initial term, notice of non-renewal must therefore be given no later than 2 November 2026.

### 5.3 Termination for Convenience

After the first 18 months of the initial term, the Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving at least six months' written notice. Acme may not terminate for convenience during the initial term.

### 5.4 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of a written notice describing the breach, or becomes insolvent. Three or more months in any rolling 12-month period in which availability falls below 99.0% is a material breach by Acme.

### 5.5 Exit Assistance

On expiry or termination for any reason, Acme will provide exit assistance for up to 90 days. Exit assistance includes exporting all Client Data in Parquet format within 30 days of the Client's request, handing over data model documentation, and keeping Atlas running for the Client in read-only mode during the exit period. Exit assistance beyond the standard export is charged at the day rates in Schedule 3.

## 6. Fees and Payment

### 6.1 Monthly Fee

The Monthly Fee for the Atlas service is EUR 38,000, invoiced monthly in advance on the first Business Day of each month. The Monthly Fee covers up to 250 named users and up to 2 TB of modelled data. Additional users are charged at EUR 60 per user per month and additional storage at EUR 400 per TB per month.

### 6.2 Onboarding Fee

A one-off onboarding fee of EUR 45,000 was invoiced on the Effective Date.

### 6.3 Indexation

On each renewal the Monthly Fee increases by the annual change in the eurozone Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices published for the preceding October, capped at 5%.

### 6.4 Invoicing and Payment

Acme issues invoices electronically from its invoicing system, Ledgerline. Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Late payments carry interest at 2% per year above the European Central Bank main refinancing rate. The Client must raise any dispute about an invoice within 15 Business Days of receiving it; undisputed amounts remain payable on time.

### 6.5 Expenses

Travel to the Client's sites in Scandinavia for the monthly insights review is included in the Monthly Fee up to four trips per year. Additional travel requested by the Client is charged at cost in line with Acme's expense policy.

## 7. Service Levels

### 7.1 Availability Commitment

Acme commits that Atlas will be available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, measured as described in Schedule 2.

### 7.2 Service Credits

If availability falls below the commitment, the Client is entitled to Service Credits calculated under Schedule 2. Service Credits are applied to the next invoice and are the Client's sole financial remedy for missed availability, without affecting the Client's termination right under clause 5.4.

### 7.3 Incident Response

Acme will acknowledge Severity 1 incidents within 30 minutes and Severity 2 incidents within two hours, at any time. Acme will give the Client status updates at least every hour during a Severity 1 incident and a written incident report within ten Business Days after any Severity 1 incident.

### 7.4 Recovery Objectives

Acme maintains a disaster recovery capability with a recovery point objective of 5 minutes and a recovery time objective of 30 minutes for the Atlas production database. Acme will test its failover procedure at least once per quarter and share a summary of the results with the Client on request.

## 8. Client Responsibilities

The Client will provide timely access to its source systems through the connectors agreed during onboarding; nominate a single point of contact for each source system; keep its own user list up to date and remove leavers within five Business Days; and ensure that Client Data supplied to Atlas does not contain special category personal data unless agreed in writing.

## 9. Data Protection

### 9.1 Roles

For personal data contained in Client Data, the Client is the controller and Acme is the processor. The Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") in Schedule 4 forms part of this Agreement. The DPA was last updated in March 2024.

### 9.2 Processing Locations

Client Data is stored and processed in the Hosting Provider's Frankfurt region, with an asynchronous replica in the Hosting Provider's Dubai region for disaster recovery only. Acme will not move Client Data to any other region without the Client's prior written consent.

### 9.3 Approved Subprocessors

The Client approves the following Subprocessors:

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Location | Client Data processed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cobalt Cloud GmbH | Hosting of Atlas (enterprise agreement CC-ENT-4471) | Frankfurt (primary), Dubai (disaster recovery) | All Client Data |
| Ledgerline Software | Invoicing | European Union | Billing contacts and usage volumes only |
| Grafana Labs | Monitoring and alerting | European Union | System metrics only; no Client Data |

### 9.4 Changes to Subprocessors

Acme will give the Client at least 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a Subprocessor. The Client may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection, the Client may terminate the affected part of the services without penalty.

### 9.5 Personal Data Breaches

Acme will notify the Client of any personal data breach affecting Client Data without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours of becoming aware of it. Acme's contract with the Hosting Provider requires the Hosting Provider to notify Acme of security incidents within 24 hours, so that Acme can meet this obligation.

## 10. Security

### 10.1 Certification

Acme will maintain ISO 27001 certification covering the Atlas service for the duration of this Agreement and will provide its certificate on request. Acme's next recertification audit is scheduled for February 2027.

### 10.2 Security Controls

Acme will encrypt Client Data at rest and in transit, restrict production access to named engineers using single sign-on with hardware-key multi-factor authentication, review production access quarterly, and commission an independent penetration test of Atlas at least once a year.

### 10.3 Audit

Once in any 12-month period, the Client may audit Acme's compliance with this clause 10 and the DPA on 20 Business Days' notice. Audits take place during business hours and are at the Client's cost.

## 11. Intellectual Property

Acme retains all intellectual property in Atlas, its software and its generic data models. The Client retains all rights in Client Data. Dashboards and data models built specifically for the Client are licensed to the Client on a perpetual, royalty-free basis for its internal use, including after termination.

## 12. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep the other Party's confidential information secret, use it only to perform this Agreement and disclose it only to employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations. These obligations survive for five years after termination.

## 13. Liability and Insurance

### 13.1 Liability Cap

Each Party's total liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement in any contract year is limited to the total fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim.

### 13.2 Exclusions from the Cap

The liability cap does not apply to breaches of clause 12 (Confidentiality), breaches of clause 9 (Data Protection), the Client's obligation to pay fees, or liability that cannot be limited by law.

### 13.3 Excluded Losses

Neither Party is liable for loss of profit, loss of business or indirect or consequential loss.

### 13.4 Insurance

Acme maintains professional indemnity insurance of at least EUR 5 million per claim and cyber insurance of at least EUR 3 million per claim.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The Parties will first try to resolve any dispute through their executive sponsors, Sara Qureshi for Acme and Erik Halvorsen for the Client, within 20 Business Days. Disputes not resolved in that time will be referred to arbitration under the rules of the London Court of International Arbitration, seated in London.

## Schedule 1 — Service Description

### S1.1 Data Sources

Atlas connects to three Client systems: the warehouse management system (inventory, picks and putaways), the transport management system (routes, vehicle telemetry and delivery events) and the order platform (customer orders and returns).

### S1.2 Dashboards

The standard dashboard set covers warehouse throughput, picking accuracy, dock-to-stock time, fleet utilisation, on-time delivery and returns analysis. Dashboards refresh every 15 minutes.

### S1.3 Monthly Insights Review

Each month an Acme consultant presents trends, anomalies and recommendations to the Client's operations leadership. The review is held in person in Bergen up to four times a year and by video call otherwise.

## Schedule 2 — Availability and Service Credits

### S2.1 Measurement

Availability is measured by Acme's external monitoring, which checks the dashboard login page and a sample dashboard from two locations every minute. A minute counts as downtime when both checks fail from both locations. Availability for a month is the number of minutes in the month minus downtime minutes, divided by the number of minutes in the month, excluding planned maintenance.

### S2.2 Service Credit Table

| Monthly availability | Service Credit (% of that month's Monthly Fee) |
|---|---|
| 99.5% or higher | No credit |
| Below 99.5% but at least 99.0% | 5% |
| Below 99.0% | 15% |

### S2.3 Cap and Claims

Service Credits in any month are capped at 25% of that month's Monthly Fee. The Client must claim a Service Credit within 60 days of the end of the month concerned.

### S2.4 Worked Example

In a 31-day month there are 44,640 minutes. An outage of 220 minutes gives availability of 99.51%, which is above 99.5%, so no Service Credit is due. An outage of 250 minutes gives availability of 99.44%, which earns a Service Credit of 5% of the Monthly Fee.

## Schedule 3 — Day Rates

Additional work under a Statement of Work, and exit assistance beyond the standard export, is charged at the following day rates: Partner EUR 2,400; Director EUR 1,900; Manager EUR 1,400; Senior Consultant EUR 1,100; Consultant EUR 850. Day rates are fixed until 31 January 2027.

## Schedule 4 — Data Processing Addendum (Summary)

It incorporates the approved Subprocessor list in clause 9.3, the 48-hour breach notification commitment in clause 9.5, and Acme's obligation to delete or return all Client Data within 30 days of the end of the exit period.

## Schedule 6 — Security Requirements

### S6.1 Access Management

Access to Client Data in Atlas is limited to named members of Acme's platform team and the consultants assigned to the Client's account. Access is granted through single sign-on with hardware-key multi-factor authentication and reviewed every quarter. Acme removes access within one Business Day when a person leaves the account team. The Client may request the current list of people with access at any time.

### S6.2 Client User Management

Client users authenticate to Atlas dashboards through the Client's own identity provider using SAML single sign-on. The Client controls which of its employees have access. Acme rotates the Atlas SAML signing certificate once a year and gives the Client at least 14 days' notice before each rotation so that the Client can update its identity provider.

### S6.3 Vulnerability Management

Acme patches critical vulnerabilities in Atlas components within 7 days and high vulnerabilities within 30 days. The annual independent penetration test covers the dashboard service, the ingestion workers and the administration console. Acme shares the executive summary of each test with the Client and confirms when critical and high findings are fixed.

### S6.4 Logging

Acme keeps security logs for Atlas, including administrator actions and authentication events, for 12 months. Logs that might contain Client Data are stored in the Frankfurt region only.

### S6.5 Business Continuity

Acme maintains a disaster recovery capability in the Hosting Provider's Dubai region, as described in clause 7.4. Failover is manual and follows Acme's database failover runbook. Acme will test failover at least once every quarter.

## Schedule 12 — Frequently Asked Questions for Acme Staff Working on the Halvorsen Account

**Where may Halvorsen data be stored?** Only in Cobalt Cloud's Frankfurt region, with the disaster recovery replica in Dubai (clause 9.2). Never copy Halvorsen data to the London region, to laptops or to other tools.

**Can we add a new tool that will see Halvorsen data?** Only after a vendor security review under Acme's procurement policy and 30 days' notice to Halvorsen under clause 9.4.

**How fast must we report a breach?** Within 48 hours of becoming aware of it (clause 9.5). Report any suspected incident internally at once through Beacon and to the General Counsel.

**What do we owe if Atlas is down?** Nothing if monthly availability stays at or above 99.5%. Below that, 5% of the month's EUR 38,000 fee, or 15% below 99.0%, capped at 25% (Schedule 2). The Client must claim within 60 days of the month end.

**Who decides on renewal?** The executive sponsors. The notice deadline for the initial term is 2 November 2026; without notice, the MSA renews for 12 months from 1 February 2027.

**Can Halvorsen leave early?** After the first 18 months, with six months' notice (clause 5.3), so from 1 August 2025 onwards.
