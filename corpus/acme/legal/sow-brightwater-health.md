# Statement of Work — Brightwater Health Trust Cloud Migration

## Parties and Framework

This Statement of Work (SOW) is between Acme Advisory Group and Brightwater Health Trust, a UK healthcare provider. It is governed by the framework agreement signed between the parties in 2025.

## Scope

Acme will migrate Brightwater's patient scheduling and reporting systems to the cloud. Hosting is provided by Cobalt Cloud as Acme's subcontractor.

## Term

The SOW runs from 1 May 2026 to 15 December 2026.

## Fees and Milestones

The fixed fee is GBP 410,000, payable on milestones:

1. Discovery complete — GBP 80,000 (due June 2026).
2. Landing zone and first system migrated — GBP 120,000 (due August 2026).
3. Second system migrated — GBP 130,000 (due October 2026).
4. Handover and hypercare complete — GBP 80,000 (due December 2026).

## Data Residency

Patient data must remain in the United Kingdom. All Brightwater workloads run in Cobalt Cloud's London region only and must not be replicated to Frankfurt or Dubai. This requirement follows UK GDPR and the Trust's data security policy.
