# ISO 27001 Recertification Audit Plan 2027

## Audit Details

Acme's ISO 27001 recertification audit will be carried out by the certification body Nordcert from 15 to 19 February 2027. The audit covers all three offices and the Atlas platform. Audit lead on Acme's side: Tomás Ferreira, General Counsel.

## Preconditions

Before the audit:

- Every active client contract must have a current data processing addendum.
- Every vendor processing client data must have an in-force agreement.
- Any contract that expires before the audit must either be renewed or have a documented exit plan.
