# Enterprise Agreement — Cobalt Cloud GmbH

## Parties

An enterprise cloud services agreement between Cobalt Cloud GmbH ("Cobalt") and Acme Advisory Group as the customer. Agreement reference: CC-ENT-4471.

## Signature

Signed on 1 September 2023 by Daniel Okafor, CFO, on behalf of Acme.

## Term

The agreement runs from 1 September 2023 to 31 December 2026. Renewal must be agreed in writing; there is no automatic renewal. Renewal negotiations are led by the finance team with technical input from Mei Lin Zhou.

## Committed Spend

Acme commits to a minimum spend of EUR 22,000 per month across all regions.

## Service Level Agreement

Cobalt commits to 99.9% monthly availability per region. Service credits:

- Availability below 99.9%: credit of 10% of that month's regional fees.
- Availability below 99.0%: credit of 25% of that month's regional fees.

Credits must be claimed in writing within 30 days of the end of the incident. Late claims are not honoured.

## Data Processing

Cobalt acts as a processor of Acme client data. Data stays in the region where it is stored unless Acme configures replication.

## Support

Priority-one support tickets receive a response within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day.
