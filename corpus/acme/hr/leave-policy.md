# Leave Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Acme Advisory Group in Karachi, Dubai and London. Contractors follow the terms in their contracts. Where local employment law gives a more generous entitlement, local law applies. The policy is owned by Priya Raman, Head of People, and summarised in the Employee Handbook.

## Annual Leave

### Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted days. Employees who join or leave during the year receive a pro-rata entitlement for the months worked, rounded up to the nearest half day.

### Accrual

Annual leave accrues at 1.83 days per completed month of service. During probation, employees may take up to three days of accrued leave with their manager's approval.

### Carryover

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited. Unused leave beyond the five-day carryover is not paid out, except on leaving Acme.

### Requesting Annual Leave

Leave is requested in PeopleDesk, the HR portal. Requests for more than three consecutive days need at least two weeks' notice. The line manager approves or declines within three business days. If you work on a client engagement, also tell your engagement manager so that client cover can be arranged.

### Busy Periods

Managers may decline leave during critical client milestones, such as a planned cutover or a regulatory deadline, but must offer alternative dates. Platform engineers on the on-call rota must arrange a swap before taking leave during their on-call week.

## Sick Leave

### Entitlement

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out on leaving.

### Reporting Sickness

Tell your line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence and record the absence in PeopleDesk when you return. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive days.

### Long-Term Sickness

If sickness lasts longer than the paid entitlement, the People team will agree next steps with you, which may include extended paid leave at the leadership team's discretion, unpaid leave or a phased return to work.

## Parental Leave

### Maternity Leave

Maternity leave is 16 weeks at full pay. Employees may take up to four weeks before the expected week of birth.

### Paternity Leave

Paternity leave is 4 weeks at full pay, to be taken within six months of the birth or adoption, in one block or in two blocks of two weeks.

### Adoption

Adoptive parents receive the same entitlements as birth parents.

### Return to Work

Employees returning from maternity leave may work reduced hours at full pay for the first four weeks back.

## Bereavement and Compassionate Leave

Employees receive 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent or sibling), and 2 days for other close relatives. Up to 3 days of paid compassionate leave a year may be granted for serious illness of an immediate family member.

## Religious Leave

Hajj leave of up to 15 working days is available once during employment, after two years of continuous service. Requests should be made at least three months in advance.

## Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days a year may be approved by the People team for personal reasons. Benefits such as health insurance continue during unpaid leave; provident fund contributions pause.

## Public Holidays

Each office observes the public holidays of its country. The People team publishes the holiday calendar for each office every December. Employees who work on a public holiday at a client's request receive a replacement day off within three months.

## Leave During the Notice Period

Employees serving notice may use their remaining annual leave with their manager's agreement. Acme may require employees to take outstanding leave during garden leave. Unused annual leave is paid in the final salary.

## Records

All leave is recorded in PeopleDesk. Line managers can see their team's leave calendar. The People team audits leave balances every quarter and reminds employees with more than ten unused days in September.

## Worked Examples

### Carryover

An employee has 8 unused days at 31 December. Five days carry over and must be used by 31 March; the other 3 days are forfeited. If the employee resigns in February, unused annual leave, including carried-over days, is paid in the final salary.

### Sickness During Annual Leave

If an employee falls ill during annual leave and provides a medical certificate covering the days, those days are recorded as sick leave instead of annual leave and the annual leave is restored.

### Leave for an On-Call Engineer

A platform engineer is on the on-call rota for the week of a planned holiday. They must arrange a swap in PagerDuty and tell the platform lead before the leave is approved, because the on-call engineer protects Atlas availability commitments to clients such as Halvorsen Logistics.

### Leave During a Client Cutover

A consultant on the Brightwater Health Trust migration requests leave over the PatientFlow cutover weekend. The engagement manager may decline because it is a critical client milestone, but must offer alternative dates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do public holidays count against annual leave?** No. Public holidays are in addition to the 22 days.

**Can I take annual leave in half days?** Yes, in PeopleDesk.

**Can I borrow leave from next year?** Up to three days, with the Head of People's approval. Borrowed days are deducted from final pay if you leave before earning them.
