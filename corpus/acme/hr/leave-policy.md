# Leave Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Acme Advisory Group in Karachi, Dubai and London. Contractors follow the terms in their contracts.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, and carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited.

### Requesting Annual Leave

Leave is requested in PeopleDesk. Requests for more than three consecutive days need at least two weeks' notice. The line manager approves or declines within three business days.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental Leave

Maternity leave is 16 weeks at full pay. Paternity leave is 4 weeks at full pay, to be taken within six months of the birth or adoption. Adoptive parents receive the same entitlements.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees receive 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member.

## Public Holidays

Each office observes the public holidays of its country. The People team publishes the holiday calendar for each office every December.
