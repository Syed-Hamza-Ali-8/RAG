# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid Working

Acme follows a hybrid model: employees work from the office at least three days per week. Teams agree their office days with their line manager. Client-site days count as office days.

## Remote Work Allowance

Employees in Karachi receive an internet allowance of PKR 8,000 per month, paid with salary. Dubai and London staff receive a local equivalent.

## Equipment

IT issues each employee a laptop and, on request, a monitor and headset for home use. Requests are raised in Beacon. Equipment remains company property and must be returned on leaving.

## Working From Abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year with prior approval from their line manager and the People team. Client data must not be accessed from countries where the client contract forbids it.

## Security Requirements

Remote work must use the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN connected. Screens must be locked when unattended.
