# Performance Review Process

## Cycle

Acme runs two reviews per year: a mid-year check-in in July and the annual review in March. The annual review rating feeds the April salary review.

## Ratings

Ratings use a five-point scale:

1. Unsatisfactory
2. Needs improvement
3. Meets expectations
4. Exceeds expectations
5. Outstanding

## Calibration

Ratings proposed by line managers are calibrated by a committee chaired by Priya Raman, Head of People. Each practice lead attends calibration for their own practice. Calibration happens in the second week of March.

## Bonus Eligibility

Employees rated 4 or 5 are eligible for an annual bonus of up to 15% of base salary. Employees rated 3 are eligible for up to 5%. Ratings of 1 or 2 receive no bonus.

## Performance Improvement Plans

An employee rated 1 or 2 is placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with written objectives and fortnightly check-ins with their manager and an HR partner.

## 2026 Annual Review Summary

In the March 2026 annual review, 212 employees were rated: 18 were rated Outstanding, 61 Exceeds expectations, 119 Meets expectations, 11 Needs improvement and 3 Unsatisfactory. Fourteen employees started a PIP.
