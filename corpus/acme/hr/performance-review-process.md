# Performance Review Process

## Purpose

The performance review process gives every employee clear feedback, links pay and promotion to performance, and identifies development needs. It is owned by Priya Raman, Head of People.

## Cycle

Acme runs two reviews per year:

- A mid-year check-in in July, focused on progress against objectives and development. No rating is given.
- The annual review in March, which produces a rating that feeds the April salary review and the annual bonus.

## Objectives

At the start of each year, each employee agrees four to six objectives with their line manager in PeopleDesk. At least one objective must relate to client delivery and one to personal development. Objectives may be updated at the mid-year check-in if client work changes.

## Gathering Feedback

### Engagement Feedback

For every client engagement lasting more than four weeks, the engagement manager records short feedback in PeopleDesk within two weeks of the employee rolling off. Annual reviews use this feedback, so employees who work on several engagements are assessed on all of them, not just the most recent.

### Peer Feedback

In February, each employee may nominate up to three colleagues to give peer feedback. Managers may add one more.

### Self-Assessment

Employees complete a self-assessment against their objectives and Acme's competency framework by the end of February.

## Ratings

Ratings use a five-point scale:

1. Unsatisfactory
2. Needs improvement
3. Meets expectations
4. Exceeds expectations
5. Outstanding

## Calibration

### Committee

Ratings proposed by line managers are calibrated by a committee chaired by Priya Raman, Head of People. Each practice lead attends calibration for their own practice: Farah Naqvi for Digital Transformation, Mei Lin Zhou for Cloud Advisory and Tomás Ferreira for Risk and Compliance.

### Timing

Calibration happens in the second week of March. Managers must not share proposed ratings with employees before calibration ends.

### Guidance

There is no forced distribution, but the committee challenges any team where more than 40% of ratings are 4 or 5, or where ratings differ sharply from engagement feedback.

## Review Meeting

After calibration, each line manager holds a review meeting with each employee before 31 March. The employee receives their final rating, written feedback, and objectives for the coming year. Employees may add comments to their review record in PeopleDesk.

## Bonus Eligibility

Employees rated 4 or 5 are eligible for an annual bonus of up to 15% of base salary. Employees rated 3 are eligible for up to 5%. Ratings of 1 or 2 receive no bonus. The bonus pool depends on the firm's results against the revenue target in the annual budget. Bonuses are paid with April salaries.

## Link to Salary Review

The April salary review uses the March rating and the employee's position in their grade's salary band. Increments range from 0% to 12%.

## Performance Improvement Plans

An employee rated 1 or 2 is placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with written objectives and fortnightly check-ins with their manager and an HR partner. At the end of the PIP the outcome is either successful completion, an extension of up to 30 days, or the start of the formal capability procedure.

## Appeals

An employee who disagrees with their rating may appeal in writing to the Head of People within ten working days of the review meeting. A practice lead not involved in the original rating reviews the appeal and responds within 15 working days. The appeal outcome is final.

## 2026 Annual Review Summary

In the March 2026 annual review, 212 employees were rated: 18 were rated Outstanding, 61 Exceeds expectations, 119 Meets expectations, 11 Needs improvement and 3 Unsatisfactory. Fourteen employees started a PIP. Seven appeals were lodged; two ratings were raised on appeal.

## Records and Confidentiality

Review records are stored in PeopleDesk and visible only to the employee, their line manager, the calibration committee and the People team. Ratings are never shared with other employees. Records are kept for six years after an employee leaves.

## Worked Example

A Senior Consultant (B2) worked on two engagements in the year: the Brightwater Health Trust migration and an Atlas onboarding for a new insurance client. Both engagement managers recorded feedback in PeopleDesk. The line manager proposes a rating of 4, Exceeds expectations, based on strong feedback from the Brightwater cutovers. At calibration in the second week of March, Mei Lin Zhou, as Cloud Advisory practice lead, confirms the rating. The employee becomes eligible for a bonus of up to 15% of base salary and an April increment depending on their position in the B2 band. If the employee's line manager also recommends promotion to Manager (C1), the promotion moves them to at least the C1 minimum with an increase of at least 10%.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can my rating change after calibration?** Only through the appeal process.

**What if I joined after October?** Employees who joined after 1 October receive a probation review instead of an annual rating, and are eligible for a pro-rata bonus at the line manager's discretion.

**Does a PIP affect my bonus?** Employees rated 1 or 2 receive no bonus, whether or not a PIP is started.
