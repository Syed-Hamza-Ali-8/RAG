# Salary Bands 2026

## Purpose

This document sets the 2026 base salary ranges for each grade at Acme Advisory Group. It is confidential and restricted to the People team. Ranges are monthly gross base salary for the Karachi office; Dubai and London use local-currency bands published separately.

## Bands (Karachi, PKR per month)

| Grade | Title | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Analyst | 180,000 | 260,000 |
| B1 | Consultant | 300,000 | 420,000 |
| B2 | Senior Consultant | 450,000 | 600,000 |
| C1 | Manager | 650,000 | 850,000 |
| C2 | Senior Manager | 900,000 | 1,200,000 |
| D1 | Director | 1,300,000 | 1,800,000 |
| D2 | Partner | Set by the board | Set by the board |

## Dubai and London Equivalents

Dubai bands are expressed in AED and London bands in GBP. For example, the Manager (C1) band is AED 32,000 to 41,000 per month in Dubai and GBP 68,000 to 84,000 per year in London.

## Annual Salary Review

Salaries are reviewed once a year in April. Increments range from 0% to 12% depending on the performance rating and the employee's position within the band. Employees above the band maximum receive a one-off lump sum instead of a base increase.

## Promotion

A promotion moves the employee to at least the minimum of the new band, with a minimum increase of 10% on base salary.

## Confidentiality

Salary bands must not be shared outside the People team and the leadership team. Individual salaries are never disclosed to other employees.
