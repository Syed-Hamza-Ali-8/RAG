# Atlas Platform Architecture

## Overview

Atlas is Acme's managed analytics platform. It ingests client operational data, models it in a warehouse and serves dashboards to client users. The platform is owned by the Cloud Advisory practice; the technical owner is Mei Lin Zhou, Chief Technology Officer. Day-to-day operations are run by the platform team under Bilal Ahmed.

Atlas's largest client is Halvorsen Logistics, whose Master Services Agreement commits Acme to 99.5% monthly availability, a recovery point objective of 5 minutes and a recovery time objective of 30 minutes.

## Hosting

### Provider and Regions

Atlas runs on Cobalt Cloud under enterprise agreement CC-ENT-4471. The primary region is Frankfurt and the secondary (disaster recovery) region is Dubai. Cobalt Cloud commits to 99.9% monthly availability per region.

### Data Residency

Client data for European clients stays in Frankfurt, with an asynchronous replica in Dubai for disaster recovery only. UK healthcare workloads, such as the Brightwater Health Trust systems migrated under a separate Statement of Work, run in a completely separate Cobalt Cloud account in the London region and never touch Atlas.

### Availability Zones

Since the March 2026 outage, the primary database is being spread across three Frankfurt availability zones (action item ATL-1125). Until that work is complete, all primary database volumes remain in zone fra-2.

## Components

### Ingestion Workers

Ingestion workers run on Cobalt's managed Kubernetes. They pull data from client systems every 15 minutes through connectors for warehouse management, transport management and order platforms. Failed pulls are retried three times and then raise an alert. Each client has its own namespace and credentials.

### Data Warehouse

A managed PostgreSQL 16 cluster stores the modelled data. It replicates asynchronously to the Dubai region; replication lag is normally under five seconds and alerts at 60 seconds. Point-in-time recovery backups are kept for 14 days.

### Dashboard Service

The dashboard service serves client users and authenticates them through single sign-on (SAML). Each client connects its own identity provider. The SAML signing certificate is rotated every year; since the July 2026 SSO outage, its expiry is monitored automatically with an alert 30 days ahead.

### Configuration Service

The configuration service holds environment settings, including the active database endpoint. During failover, the endpoint is switched to Dubai here. Clients cache the endpoint for 30 seconds.

## Billing Integration with Ledgerline

Invoices for Atlas subscriptions are generated by Ledgerline, Acme's invoicing and expense system. Every night at 02:00 UTC, Atlas writes a billing export with named-user counts and storage volumes per client to an SFTP drop that Ledgerline reads. Ledgerline uses the export to add charges for users above 250 or storage above 2 TB, as allowed by the Halvorsen MSA. Ledgerline receives only billing contacts and usage volumes, never client operational data, which is why it appears as a limited subprocessor in the Halvorsen data processing addendum.

## Observability

Grafana Cloud provides metrics, logs and alerting. Alerts route to PagerDuty, which pages the platform on-call engineer. Grafana Labs receives only system metrics, not client data. External synthetic monitoring checks the dashboard login page and a sample dashboard every minute from two locations; this measurement is the basis for client availability reporting.

## Deployment

Code is hosted in GitHub Enterprise and deployed by GitHub Actions. Production deployments need one approving review and run only between 10:00 and 16:00 PKT on weekdays. Platform upgrades follow a checklist that, since April 2026, includes reviewing every runbook affected by the change.

## Security

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Production access requires SSO with hardware-key multi-factor authentication. Access is reviewed quarterly by the platform team. An independent penetration test of Atlas is commissioned every year, as required by client contracts and by Acme's ISO 27001 certification.

## Recovery

The database failover runbook describes how to promote the Dubai replica. Failover drills run every quarter; the Q2 2026 drill completed in 19 minutes and the Q3 2026 drill in 24 minutes, both within the 30-minute recovery time objective.

## Known Limitations

- Single-zone primary database until ATL-1125 is complete.
- Staging database configuration differs from production (ATL-1127, not started).
- Cross-region failover is manual; there is no automatic promotion.
