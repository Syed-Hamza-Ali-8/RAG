# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how Acme employees may work away from the office while keeping client data secure and teams connected. It applies to all employees. Contractors follow the terms of their contracts and client site rules.

## Hybrid Working

### Office Days

Acme follows a hybrid model: employees work from the office at least three days per week. Teams agree their office days with their line manager, and each team should share at least one common office day a week. Client-site days count as office days.

### New Joiners

During probation, new joiners work from the office at least four days a week so they can learn from colleagues and their onboarding buddy.

### Exceptions

Line managers may agree temporary exceptions for up to four weeks, for example after an injury. Longer exceptions need a formal flexible working request through the People team.

## Remote Work Allowance

Employees in Karachi receive an internet allowance of PKR 8,000 per month, paid with salary. Dubai and London staff receive a local equivalent set each January. The allowance stops if an employee moves to a fully office-based arrangement.

## Equipment

### Standard Equipment

IT issues each employee a laptop and, on request, a monitor, keyboard and headset for home use. Requests are raised in Beacon, the IT helpdesk. Equipment remains company property and must be returned on leaving.

### Home Workstation

Employees should complete the home workstation self-assessment in PeopleDesk within their first month. The People team can arrange an ergonomic chair for employees with a medical need.

## Working From Abroad

### Allowance

Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year with prior approval from their line manager and the People team. Approval requests should be raised at least two weeks in advance.

### Client Restrictions

Client data must not be accessed from countries where the client contract forbids it. For example, work on the Brightwater Health Trust migration involves UK patient data under strict residency terms, and engineers on that engagement must get written confirmation from the engagement lead before working from outside the UK, Pakistan or the UAE.

### Tax and Immigration

Employees are responsible for checking visa rules for the country they visit. The People team will refuse requests that could create tax or immigration problems for the employee or for Acme.

## Security Requirements

### Network

Remote work must use the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN connected. Never use shared or public computers for Acme work.

### Physical Security

Screens must be locked when unattended. Client documents must not be printed at home. Video calls about client matters should be taken where others cannot overhear them.

### Production Access

Engineers with production access to Atlas must use their hardware security key and may only access production from an Acme-issued laptop. Production access from abroad is allowed only for the on-call engineer during an incident.

## Availability and Communication

Employees working remotely are expected to be reachable on Slack during core hours (9:00 to 18:00 local time) and to keep their calendar up to date. Cameras should be on for client meetings unless the client prefers otherwise.

## Reviewing This Policy

The People team reviews this policy every year, taking into account office occupancy data and employee feedback.

## Costs

Acme does not pay for home office furniture other than ergonomic equipment needed for medical reasons, or for home utilities beyond the internet allowance. Travel between home and the office is not reimbursed. Travel to a client site from home is reimbursed under the Expense Policy where it costs more than travel to the office.

## Breaches of This Policy

Accessing client data from an unapproved country, or without the VPN, is a serious breach of this policy and of client contracts. It may lead to disciplinary action and must be reported to the General Counsel, who will decide whether the client needs to be notified.

## Worked Examples

**An engineer on the Atlas team wants to work from home five days a week for a month after surgery.** The line manager may agree a temporary exception for up to four weeks. If more time is needed, the engineer submits a flexible working request through the People team.

**A consultant on the Brightwater engagement wants to work from Spain for two weeks.** Working from abroad is allowed for up to 30 days a year with approval from the line manager and the People team. Because the Brightwater SOW involves UK patient data, the consultant also needs written confirmation from the engagement lead, Mei Lin Zhou, before accessing Trust systems from outside the UK, Pakistan or the UAE.

**The on-call engineer is travelling when Atlas goes down.** Production access from abroad is allowed only for the on-call engineer during an incident, using an Acme laptop, the VPN and a hardware security key.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I choose my office days?** Yes, with your line manager's agreement and at least one common team day.

**Does a client site day count as an office day?** Yes.

**Is the internet allowance taxable?** It is paid with salary and treated according to local tax rules; the People team can explain the treatment in each country.

**Can I work from a café?** Only with the VPN connected, and never for calls about client matters where others can overhear.
