# Acme Employee Handbook

## 1. Welcome

### 1.1 About This Handbook

This handbook explains how employment at Acme Advisory Group works: your contract, working hours, pay and benefits, leave, conduct, security, performance and what happens when you leave. It applies to all permanent employees in Karachi, Dubai and London. Where local law gives you more favourable rights, local law applies. The handbook is owned by the People team, led by Priya Raman, Head of People, and is reviewed every January.

### 1.2 Where to Find Help

- HR questions, leave, payroll and benefits: PeopleDesk, the HR portal.
- IT equipment, accounts and access: Beacon, the IT helpdesk, or extension 4357 for urgent issues.
- Expenses and travel: Ledgerline, the finance system for invoicing and expense claims.
- Ethics and conduct concerns: Tomás Ferreira, General Counsel, or the anonymous ethics line.

## 2. Your Employment

### 2.1 Contracts

Every employee receives a written contract stating their grade, salary, office location, notice period and start date. Grades run from A1 (Analyst) to D2 (Partner). Salary bands for each grade are confidential and held by the People team.

### 2.2 Probation

New employees serve a probation period of three months. Your line manager holds a probation review in the final two weeks. Probation can be extended once, by up to three months, if more time is needed to assess performance. During probation either side may end employment with one month's notice.

### 2.3 Notice Periods After Probation

| Grade | Notice period |
|---|---|
| A1 Analyst to B2 Senior Consultant | Two months |
| C1 Manager and C2 Senior Manager | Three months |
| D1 Director and D2 Partner | Six months |

Acme may ask you not to work some or all of your notice period ("garden leave") and will continue to pay your salary and benefits during that time.

### 2.4 Right to Work and Background Checks

Offers are conditional on proof of the right to work in the country of employment, verification of qualifications and two references. Employees who will have production access to Atlas or to client data also complete a criminal record check where local law allows.

## 3. Working Hours and Location

### 3.1 Core Hours

Core hours are 9:00 to 18:00 local time, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. In Karachi and Dubai a 12:30 to 14:00 break is allowed on Fridays for prayers.

### 3.2 Hybrid Working

Acme follows a hybrid model. You work from the office at least three days a week, agreed with your line manager. Days at a client site count as office days. The full rules, including working from abroad for up to 30 days a year, are in the Remote Work Policy.

### 3.3 Overtime

Consultants are not paid overtime but may take time off in lieu (TOIL) for weekend work requested by a client, up to five days per year, with manager approval. Engineers on the platform on-call rota receive an on-call allowance instead, as described in the on-call runbook.

### 3.4 Client Site Work

When working at a client site, follow the client's site rules and security procedures as well as Acme's. Report any conflict between the two to your engagement manager.

## 4. Pay and Benefits

### 4.1 Payroll

Salaries are paid monthly. Payslips are published in PeopleDesk on the 25th of each month, and salary is credited on the last working day of the month. Report payroll errors to the People team within ten working days.

### 4.2 Salary Review and Bonus

Salaries are reviewed once a year in April, after the March annual performance review. Annual bonuses depend on your performance rating: up to 15% of base salary for ratings of 4 or 5, and up to 5% for a rating of 3.

### 4.3 Health Insurance

All employees and their spouses and children are covered by group health insurance from day one. In Karachi the annual family limit is PKR 1.5 million for inpatient care and PKR 150,000 for outpatient care. Dubai and London employees are covered by local plans that meet or exceed statutory requirements.

### 4.4 Retirement Savings

Karachi employees join the provident fund: you contribute 8.33% of base salary and Acme matches it. Dubai employees receive the end-of-service gratuity required by UAE law. London employees are enrolled in a workplace pension with an employer contribution of 6%.

### 4.5 Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of USD 1,200 for courses, books and certification exams. Certifications relevant to client work, such as cloud provider certifications and ISO 27001 lead implementer courses, may be funded beyond this budget with the practice lead's approval.

### 4.6 Internet Allowance

Karachi employees receive an internet allowance of PKR 8,000 per month for home working. Dubai and London employees receive a local equivalent.

## 5. Leave

This section summarises leave entitlements. The Leave Policy has the full rules and the request process.

### 5.1 Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to five unused days can be carried over and must be used by 31 March of the following year.

### 5.2 Sick Leave

You receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive days. Tell your line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

### 5.3 Family Leave

Maternity leave is 16 weeks at full pay. Paternity leave is 4 weeks at full pay, taken within six months of the birth or adoption. Adoptive parents receive the same entitlements.

### 5.4 Other Leave

Bereavement leave is five paid days for an immediate family member. Hajj leave of up to 15 days is available once during employment after two years of service. Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be approved by the People team.

## 6. Conduct

### 6.1 Code of Conduct

All employees must follow the Code of Conduct, which covers integrity, conflicts of interest, gifts and hospitality (gifts over USD 50 must be declared) and client confidentiality.

### 6.2 Client Confidentiality

Client information is shared only with colleagues who need it for their work and is kept in approved systems. Never discuss client names, data or work in public places or on social media. Some client contracts, such as Statements of Work with healthcare clients, have additional data residency rules; your engagement manager will brief you.

### 6.3 Social Media

You may say that you work at Acme, but do not post about clients, engagements or internal matters. Only the leadership team may speak to the media on Acme's behalf.

### 6.4 Outside Work

You must get written approval from your practice lead before taking any paid outside work, directorship or advisory role, to avoid conflicts of interest.

## 7. Information Security

### 7.1 Training

Information security training must be completed within 14 days of joining and then every year. Engineers with production access also complete secure engineering training.

### 7.2 Devices and Accounts

Use only your Acme-issued laptop for client work. Keep the operating system updated, never disable disk encryption, and lock your screen when you step away. Personal devices may be used only for email and chat through the approved mobile apps.

### 7.3 Passwords and Multi-Factor Authentication

All Acme accounts use single sign-on with multi-factor authentication. Production systems such as Atlas require a hardware security key. Never share credentials or approve a login prompt you did not start.

### 7.4 Reporting Incidents

Report any suspected security incident, lost device or phishing email immediately through Beacon or extension 4357. Acme's contracts with clients require fast breach notification, for example within 48 hours to Halvorsen Logistics, so early internal reporting matters.

## 8. Performance and Development

### 8.1 Reviews

Acme runs a mid-year check-in in July and an annual review in March, rated on a five-point scale. Ratings are calibrated by a committee chaired by the Head of People. The Performance Review Process has the full details.

### 8.2 Promotion

Promotions are decided once a year with the April salary review. A promotion moves you to at least the minimum of the new grade's band, with a minimum increase of 10% on base salary.

### 8.3 Performance Improvement

An employee rated 1 or 2 is placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan with written objectives and fortnightly check-ins.

## 9. Grievances and Discipline

### 9.1 Raising a Grievance

Raise concerns about your work, colleagues or treatment with your line manager first. If that is not appropriate, raise a formal grievance in writing with the People team, who will hold a meeting within ten working days and respond in writing. You may appeal to a practice lead not involved in the matter.

### 9.2 Disciplinary Procedure

Acme follows a fair procedure: investigation, a disciplinary meeting where you may be accompanied by a colleague, a written outcome and a right of appeal. Outcomes range from a written warning to dismissal. Gross misconduct, such as theft, serious breach of client confidentiality or deliberate security violations, may lead to dismissal without notice.

### 9.3 Speaking Up

Concerns about wrongdoing can be raised confidentially with the General Counsel or through the anonymous ethics line. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

## 10. Leaving Acme

### 10.1 Resignation

Resign in writing to your line manager and the People team. Your notice period is in your contract and summarised in section 2.3.

### 10.2 Handover and Return of Equipment

Before your last day, hand over your client work to your engagement manager and return your laptop, security key and access card to IT through Beacon. Access to Acme systems is removed at 18:00 on your last day.

### 10.3 Exit Interview and Final Pay

The People team offers an exit interview. Final pay, including any unused annual leave, is paid in the next payroll run after your last day.

## 11. Travel and Expenses

### 11.1 Booking Travel

Business travel is booked through the finance team's travel desk using the travel request form in Ledgerline, after line-manager approval. Flights under six hours are economy class; longer flights may be premium economy.

### 11.2 Claiming Expenses

Submit expense claims with receipts in Ledgerline within 30 days. Claims older than 60 days are not reimbursed. Expenses for client work must be tagged with the client engagement code so they can be recharged where the contract allows. The Expense Policy sets out approval limits and per diem rates.

## 12. Public Holidays

Each office observes the public holidays of its country. The People team publishes the holiday calendar for each office every December. If a client engagement requires you to work on a public holiday, you receive a replacement day off within three months.

## 13. Wellbeing

### 13.1 Employee Assistance Programme

All employees and their immediate families can use a confidential employee assistance programme offering counselling, legal and financial advice. Contact details are in PeopleDesk.

### 13.2 Workload

If your workload is consistently above 50 hours a week, raise it with your engagement manager or line manager. Practice leads review utilisation monthly and rebalance staffing where needed.

### 13.3 Health and Safety

Each office has a trained first aider and fire warden. Report accidents and near misses to the office manager on the same day. Employees working from home should complete the home workstation self-assessment in PeopleDesk during their first month.

## 14. Flexible Working Requests

Employees with at least six months' service may request a permanent change to their hours, days or work location. The People team responds within four weeks. Requests are approved unless there is a clear business reason, such as client coverage requirements, which will be explained in writing.

## 15. Diversity and Inclusion

Acme recruits, promotes and pays on merit and does not tolerate discrimination or harassment on any grounds. Concerns can be raised through the grievance procedure or the ethics line. The People team publishes gender and grade representation data to the leadership team every year.

## 16. Employee Data

Acme processes employee personal data for payroll, benefits, performance management and legal compliance. Employee records are held in PeopleDesk and accessed only by the People team, payroll and your line manager where needed. You can request a copy of your personal data through the People team.

## 17. References

Only the People team may give employment references on Acme's behalf. References confirm your dates of employment, grade and job title. Personal references given by colleagues must state that they are personal and not given on behalf of Acme.

## 18. Changes to This Handbook

The People team reviews this handbook every January and announces changes by email and in PeopleDesk at least 30 days before they take effect, unless a change is required by law sooner.

## 19. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I carry over more than five days of annual leave?

No. Up to five days carry over and must be used by 31 March. Exceptions are only made when a client emergency prevented you from taking leave, and require approval from your practice lead and the Head of People.

### Who approves my working-from-abroad request?

Your line manager and the People team. Allow at least two weeks. Some client contracts forbid accessing client data from certain countries, so your engagement manager must also confirm.

### What happens to my learning budget if I leave?

Unused budget is not paid out. If Acme paid more than USD 1,000 for a certification exam or course in the 12 months before you resign, you may have to repay part of the cost, as stated in the course approval form.

### How do I update my bank details or emergency contact?

Update them yourself in PeopleDesk. Bank detail changes made after the 20th of the month take effect from the following month's payroll.

## 20. Learning and Development

### 20.1 Annual Learning Plan

Each employee agrees a learning plan with their line manager in April, after the annual review. The plan lists the courses, certifications and on-the-job experience the employee will aim for in the year, and how the USD 1,200 learning budget will be used.

### 20.2 Certifications Valued by Practice

| Practice | Certifications supported |
|---|---|
| Cloud Advisory | Cloud provider architect and engineering certifications; Kubernetes administrator; PostgreSQL administration |
| Risk and Compliance | ISO 27001 lead implementer and lead auditor; privacy certifications |

### 20.3 Internal Academy

Acme runs an internal academy with monthly sessions on consulting skills, client communication, Atlas and data modelling, and secure engineering. Attendance counts towards the learning plan.

## 21. Office Life

### 21.1 Karachi

The Karachi headquarters hosts the Digital Transformation practice, the People team and finance. Lunch is subsidised on office days, and prayer rooms are available on every floor.

### 21.2 Dubai

The Dubai office is home to the Cloud Advisory practice and the Atlas operations team, and hosts the secondary operations room used during failover drills. Staff working on Atlas incidents outside office hours may use the office at any time with their access card.

### 21.3 London

The London office serves UK healthcare clients and hosts the Risk and Compliance practice. Engineers working on the Brightwater Health Trust migration are based here or visit the Trust's sites in the north of England.

## 22. Acceptable Use of IT

### 22.1 General Use

Acme systems are for business use. Limited personal use is acceptable if it does not interfere with work, does not involve client data and does not break any law or Acme policy.

### 22.2 Prohibited Use

Never install unapproved software on your laptop, connect unapproved storage devices, forward client data to personal email, or use public AI tools with client data. Requests for new software go through Beacon and, where the tool would process client data, through the vendor security review in the procurement policy.

### 22.3 Monitoring

Acme monitors its systems for security purposes, including email filtering, endpoint protection and production access logs. Monitoring is proportionate and carried out in line with local law.

## 23. Client Engagements and Confidential Information

### 23.1 Before Joining an Engagement

Your engagement manager will brief you on the client's contract terms that affect your work: data residency, permitted locations, subprocessors and notification duties. For example, the Halvorsen Logistics MSA keeps client data in Frankfurt with disaster recovery in Dubai, while the Brightwater Health Trust SOW requires all patient data to stay in the UK.

### 23.2 Leaving an Engagement

When you leave an engagement, return or delete any client materials you hold outside approved systems, and record your handover in the engagement workspace.

### 23.3 Conflicts Across Clients

Do not use information from one client's engagement on another client's engagement. If you work on engagements for two clients in the same sector, tell your practice lead so that information barriers can be put in place.

## 25. Worked Examples

### 25.1 Planning Leave Around a Client Cutover

A consultant on the Brightwater Health Trust migration wants two weeks of annual leave in October 2026, when PatientFlow is cut over. Leave of more than three days needs two weeks' notice and line-manager approval, and the engagement manager must arrange cover. Because the cutover is a critical client milestone, the manager may decline and offer alternative dates, for example in late November during handover.

### 25.2 Working From Abroad While on an Engagement

An engineer working on Atlas for Halvorsen Logistics plans to spend three weeks in Turkey visiting family and work remotely. Working from abroad is allowed for up to 30 days a year with approval from the line manager and the People team. The engineer must use the VPN, may not access production from abroad unless on call during an incident, and must check with the engagement lead that the Halvorsen MSA does not restrict access from that country.

### 25.3 Reporting a Lost Laptop

A consultant leaves their laptop in a taxi in Dubai. They report it immediately in Beacon or on extension 4357. IT remotely locks and wipes the device. Because the laptop is encrypted, client data is protected, but the General Counsel decides whether any client must be notified under its contract, such as the 48-hour breach notification commitment to Halvorsen.

### 25.4 Accepting a Gift From a Client

At the end of a project a client gives a consultant a watch worth about USD 200. Gifts above USD 50 must be declared to the compliance team within five business days and may need to be returned. The consultant declares it, and the compliance team decides whether it can be kept.

### 25.5 Claiming Expenses for a Client Visit to Bergen

A consultant travels to Bergen for Halvorsen's monthly insights review. Under the Halvorsen MSA, up to four trips a year are included in the Monthly Fee, so the trip is not recharged to the client. The consultant books through the travel desk and claims expenses in Ledgerline within 30 days, tagged with the Halvorsen engagement code.

## 27. Glossary

- Atlas: Acme's managed analytics platform, hosted on Cobalt Cloud.
- Beacon: the internal IT helpdesk portal.
- Ledgerline: the finance system used for invoicing, expenses and travel requests.
- PeopleDesk: the HR portal for leave, payroll and reviews.
- TOIL: time off in lieu.
- Engagement manager: the person running a client engagement day to day.
- Practice lead: the head of one of Acme's three practices (Digital Transformation, Cloud Advisory, Risk and Compliance).
- Garden leave: a notice period during which you are paid but asked not to work.
