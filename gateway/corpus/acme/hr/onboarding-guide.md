# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

## Before Day One

The People team sends the offer pack, a PeopleDesk login and the start-date schedule. IT prepares a laptop and accounts.

## Day One

New joiners collect their laptop from IT, sign the IT acceptable-use agreement and receive accounts for Google Workspace, Slack, Jira and Beacon. Each new joiner is assigned a buddy from their practice for the first three months.

## First Two Weeks

Mandatory information security training must be completed within 14 days of joining. New joiners in the Cloud Advisory practice also complete Atlas platform orientation.

## Probation

Probation lasts three months. The line manager holds a probation review in the final two weeks. Probation can be extended once, by up to three months.

## Useful Contacts

- People team: PeopleDesk
- IT helpdesk: Beacon, or extension 4357
- Finance (expenses and travel): Ledgerline
