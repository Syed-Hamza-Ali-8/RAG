# Office Locations

## Karachi (Headquarters)

Floor 7, Crescent Business Centre, Karachi. Around 150 staff. The Karachi office hosts the Digital Transformation practice, the People team and the finance team. Reception is staffed from 8:30 to 18:30.

## Dubai

Level 12, Harbour Tower, Dubai Marina. Around 55 staff. Home of the Cloud Advisory practice and the Atlas operations team. The Dubai office also hosts the secondary Atlas operations room used during failover exercises.

## London

3rd Floor, 22 Aldgate Row, London. Around 35 staff. The London office serves UK healthcare clients and hosts the Risk and Compliance practice.

## Visiting Another Office

Staff visiting another office should book a desk through the facilities page at least two business days in advance. Building access cards are issued by the receiving office's facilities coordinator.
