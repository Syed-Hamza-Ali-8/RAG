# Expense Policy

## Submitting Claims

Expense claims are submitted in Ledgerline with receipts within 30 days of the expense. Claims submitted later than 60 days are not reimbursed.

## Approval Limits

- Up to USD 500: line manager.
- USD 500 to USD 5,000: practice lead.
- Above USD 5,000: Chief Financial Officer (Daniel Okafor).

## Travel

Economy class for flights under six hours; premium economy for longer flights. Hotels are booked through the finance team's travel desk. Per diems: USD 60 per day in Pakistan, USD 120 in the UAE and USD 110 in the UK.

## Client-Rechargeable Expenses

Expenses that are recharged to a client must be tagged with the client's engagement code in Ledgerline.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Alcohol, personal entertainment, fines and upgrades booked without approval are not reimbursed.
