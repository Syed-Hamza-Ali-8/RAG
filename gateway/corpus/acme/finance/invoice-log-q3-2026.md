## Notes

### Cobalt Cloud SLA Credit

The July Cobalt Cloud invoice includes an SLA credit of EUR 1,650 for the 14 March 2026 Frankfurt outage. This is 10% of March's Frankfurt Regional Fees of EUR 16,500, because Frankfurt availability in March was 99.51%, below the 99.9% commitment in enterprise agreement CC-ENT-4471. The claim was filed on 3 April 2026, within the 30-day claim window that ended on 13 April, and approved on 28 April. Without the credit, the July invoice would have been EUR 23,600.

### No Credit Owed to Halvorsen

Atlas availability for Halvorsen in March 2026 was also 99.51%, above the 99.5% commitment in their MSA, so no Service Credit was owed to Halvorsen and the March, April and later Atlas invoices were issued in full.

### Late Payment by Halvorsen

Halvorsen paid the September Atlas invoice on 12 October, 11 days after the 30-day due date, because of a change in Halvorsen's accounts payable system. Under the MSA, late payments carry interest at 2% per year above the ECB main refinancing rate. The finance team decided not to charge interest on this occasion and recorded the decision in Ledgerline. Marit Lunde, Halvorsen's procurement manager, confirmed the new system is now set up correctly.

### Additional Users

The nightly Atlas billing export showed Halvorsen at 262 named users in August, 12 above the 250 included in the Monthly Fee. Ledgerline charged EUR 60 per additional user per month, as set out in the MSA, totalling EUR 720.
