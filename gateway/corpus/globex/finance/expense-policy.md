# Expense Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Globex reimburses, how to claim them and who approves them. It applies to all employees and partners. It is owned by Laura Bennett, CFO.

## Submitting Claims

Claims are submitted in Claimly, the firm's expense app, within 45 days of the expense, with receipts. Claims submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed. Photograph paper receipts in the app; keep the originals for three months in case of an audit.

## Approval Limits

- Up to USD 300: line manager.
- USD 300 to USD 3,000: director or partner.
- Above USD 3,000: Chief Financial Officer (Laura Bennett).

Approvers must not approve their own claims or claims for events they attended with the claimant; such claims go to the next level.

## Travel

### Flights

Economy class for all flights under eight hours. Premium economy may be booked for longer flights with partner approval. Flights are booked through the finance team, not by individuals.

### Hotels

Hotels are booked through the finance team within the nightly limits published on the intranet for each city.

### Per Diems

| Location | Per diem (USD per day) |
|---|---|
| Pakistan | 50 |
| UAE | 110 |
| United Kingdom | 100 |

Per diems cover meals and incidentals. Do not claim meals separately on days you claim a per diem.

### Local Travel

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed for client visits and for travel home after 22:00. During busy season, transport home after 22:00 is arranged by the office instead.

## Busy Season Meals

During busy season (15 January to 31 March), the firm provides overtime meals for staff working after 20:00 or on Saturdays. Where a team works at a client site, the engagement manager may claim team meals up to USD 15 per person per meal.

## Client Recharges

Expenses recharged to clients must quote the engagement code. Engagement letters set out what may be recharged; for example, the Marlowe Retail Group letter allows travel and subsistence for store visits and inventory counts at cost, estimated at GBP 9,000 for the FY2026 audit.

## Exam and Training Costs

Exam and tuition fees for ACCA, ICAEW or ICAP are paid directly by HR, not claimed as expenses. Professional membership fees for one body are paid by the firm.

## Gifts and Entertainment

Client entertainment must be approved in advance by the engagement partner and must not be lavish. Entertainment of audit clients is limited to modest meals connected with business meetings, in line with independence rules. Gifts to clients above USD 25 need Ethics Partner approval.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Alcohol, personal entertainment, fines, upgrades booked without approval, and personal travel combined with business trips (beyond the cost of the business trip alone) are not reimbursed.

## Misuse

False or inflated claims are gross misconduct and may lead to dismissal and reporting to professional bodies.

## Mileage

Staff who use their own car for client visits may claim mileage at PKR 45 per kilometre in Pakistan, AED 1.5 per kilometre in the UAE, or GBP 0.45 per mile in the UK, plus parking and tolls. Mileage is not paid for travel between home and the office.

## Corporate Cards

Partners and directors may hold a corporate card. All card transactions must be matched to receipts in Claimly within 30 days. Personal use of a corporate card is not allowed, even if repaid.

## Audit of Claims

Finance checks a random sample of 5% of claims each month, and all claims above USD 3,000. Findings are reported to the CFO.

## Questions

Questions about this policy go to the finance team through the expenses mailbox. The CFO decides any case the policy does not cover.

## Example: Inventory Count at a Marlowe Store

A Manchester Senior Associate attends the year-end inventory count at a Marlowe Retail Group store on 31 December. They drive 140 miles in total and claim GBP 63 in mileage plus parking in Claimly, tagged with the Marlowe engagement code, because the engagement letter allows travel for inventory counts to be recharged at cost. The count falls on a public holiday, so the employee also receives a replacement day off after busy season.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I claim a meal on a day I claim a per diem?** No; the per diem covers meals.

**Who approves a partner's expenses?** Another partner, or the CFO above USD 3,000.

**How quickly are claims paid?** Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.
