# Employee FAQ

## What are standard office hours?

Office hours are 9:30 to 17:30 local time, Monday to Friday. During busy season (15 January to 31 March) audit teams may work extended hours and Saturdays, with overtime meals provided.

## How do I get IT help?

Email the IT service desk or call extension 2020. The service desk is staffed from 8:00 to 20:00 during busy season and 9:00 to 18:00 otherwise.

## How do I claim expenses?

Expenses are claimed in Claimly, the firm's expense app. See the Expense Policy for limits.

## Where are policies kept?

Policies are on the intranet under "Firm Policies". HR policies are also available from the HR team.

## When is payroll paid?

Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month.

## Can I use personal devices for work?

Personal phones may be used for Microsoft 365 email and Teams through the managed app only. Client files must never be downloaded to personal devices.
