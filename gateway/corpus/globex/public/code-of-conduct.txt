Globex Partners LLP requires every partner and employee to uphold professional ethics, independence and client confidentiality.

Independence: staff must not hold shares or financial interests in audit clients. Any family relationship with a client's directors must be declared to the Ethics Partner before joining an engagement.

Gifts and hospitality: gifts worth up to USD 25 may be accepted. Anything above USD 25 must be declared to the Ethics Partner and is normally declined.

Confidentiality: client information may only be used for the engagement it was provided for.

Speaking up: concerns may be raised with the Legal Counsel, Henrik Dahl, or the Ethics Partner. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

Breaches may result in disciplinary action, reporting to professional bodies and dismissal.
