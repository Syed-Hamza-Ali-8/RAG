# Audit Engagement Letter — Marlowe Retail Group plc

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Parties

This engagement letter is between Globex Partners LLP ("Globex", "we" or "us"), an accounting and audit firm with offices in Lahore, Abu Dhabi and Manchester, and Marlowe Retail Group plc ("Marlowe", "the Company" or "you"), a UK retailer operating 186 stores and an online business.

### 1.2 Purpose

This letter confirms our appointment as statutory auditor of Marlowe Retail Group plc and its subsidiaries for the financial year ending 31 December 2026, and sets out the scope of our work, our respective responsibilities, the timetable, our fees and our terms of business.

### 1.3 Signature

This letter was signed on 2 March 2026 by Imran Siddiqui, Managing Partner, for Globex, and by Hannah Price, Chief Financial Officer, for Marlowe, on behalf of the board of directors. It was approved by Marlowe's audit committee, chaired by Peter Lawson, on 26 February 2026.

### 1.4 Continuing Relationship

Globex has audited Marlowe since the financial year ended 31 December 2023. This letter replaces the letter for the FY2025 audit, which ended when the FY2025 audit report was signed on 1 April 2026.

## 2. Scope of the Audit

### 2.1 Statutory Audit

We will audit the consolidated and parent company financial statements of Marlowe for the year ending 31 December 2026, in accordance with International Standards on Auditing (UK) and applicable law. Our objective is to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the financial statements as a whole are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report that includes our opinion.

### 2.2 Subsidiaries

The group audit covers Marlowe Stores Limited, Marlowe Online Limited and Marlowe Property Limited. Statutory audits of these subsidiaries are included in the scope and fee.

### 2.3 Other Information

We will read the other information in the annual report, including the strategic report and directors' report, and report any material inconsistencies with the financial statements or our knowledge obtained in the audit.

### 2.4 Out of Scope

This engagement does not include tax compliance, advice on systems implementation, or any non-audit services. Any non-audit services would require a separate engagement letter and prior approval by the audit committee under the applicable ethical standards.

## 3. Responsibilities

### 3.1 Directors' Responsibilities

The directors are responsible for preparing financial statements that give a true and fair view, for internal controls they judge necessary, for keeping adequate accounting records, and for providing us with access to all information relevant to the preparation of the financial statements.

### 3.2 Our Responsibilities

We are responsible for forming and expressing an opinion on the financial statements. An audit is not designed to detect all fraud or error, and our work should not be relied upon to disclose all misstatements or irregularities.

### 3.3 Management Representations

At the end of the audit we will ask the directors to confirm in writing certain representations made to us during the audit.

## 7. Information Exchange and Data Protection

### 7.1 Ledger Vault

All documents, including trial balances, contracts, bank confirmations and audit evidence, are exchanged through Ledger Vault, Globex's secure client document portal. Marlowe users log in with an email one-time passcode. Files must not be sent by ordinary email.

### 7.2 Data Location

Marlowe's files are stored in Ledger Vault's London storage only. Ledger Vault is hosted by Tessera Cloud Ltd under Globex's agreement TC-7720; Tessera Cloud stores UK client files in a dedicated bucket in its London region, separate from the Bahrain primary region used for other clients. Audit staff in Lahore access the files remotely through Ledger Vault; files are not copied to local devices.

### 7.3 Working Papers

Audit working papers are prepared in Fernbook, our audit working-paper platform, which links to evidence held in Ledger Vault. Working papers belong to Globex and are retained for eight years after the audit report date, as required by professional regulations.

### 7.4 Personal Data

To the extent we process personal data in performing the audit, we do so as an independent controller, as required by our professional obligations. Our privacy notice explains how we handle personal data. Our hosting provider, Tessera Cloud, is contractually required to notify us of security incidents within 12 hours, and we will notify you without undue delay, and within 72 hours, of any personal data breach affecting your data.

### 7.5 Security

Access to Marlowe's files in Ledger Vault is limited to the engagement team. Globex staff use firm-managed laptops with single sign-on through Microsoft Entra ID and number-matching multi-factor authentication.

## Appendix H — Planned Audit Response to Key Risks

### H.1 Revenue Recognition for Online Sales and Returns

Marlowe recognises online revenue on delivery and records a provision for expected returns. We will test the cut-off of online orders around 31 December 2026 by matching a sample of orders shipped in the last and first five days of the year to courier delivery records. We will test the returns provision by comparing the prior year's provision with actual returns in January and February 2026, and by recalculating the 2026 provision using Marlowe's return rates by product category. Data extracts from the online platform will be uploaded to Ledger Vault and analysed in Fernbook.

### H.2 Inventory in Stores Scheduled for Closure

Marlowe plans to close 14 stores in 2027. We will attend inventory counts at 12 sampled stores, including at least three scheduled for closure, and test whether inventory in those stores is valued at the lower of cost and net realisable value, taking into account expected clearance discounts.

### H.3 Impairment of Right-of-Use Assets

We will review management's impairment model for underperforming stores, challenge the cash flow forecasts against 2026 actual results, and test the discount rate with the help of our valuation specialists in the Manchester office.

### H.4 Management Override

We will test journal entries using data analytics across the full population of 2026 journals, focusing on manual entries posted after 31 December, entries posted by senior management, and entries to revenue and provisions.

### H.5 IT General Controls Over the New Point-of-Sale System

The new point-of-sale system went live in May 2026. Our IT audit specialists will test access controls, change management and the interface that sends daily sales from stores to the general ledger. If IT general controls are not effective, we will increase substantive testing of store revenue, which may affect the fee under section 6.3.

## Appendix J — Resilience Arrangements for Fieldwork

Because Ledger Vault was unavailable for more than six hours on 30 March 2026, during the final day of the FY2025 fieldwork, we have agreed the following arrangements for FY2026:

- Final fieldwork will finish by 26 March 2027, leaving at least three weeks before the audit committee clearance meeting on 20 April 2027.
- Globex's IT team will complete its busy-season capacity review of Ledger Vault in December 2026, including a restore test at peak data volumes.
- If Ledger Vault is unavailable for more than four hours during final fieldwork, the engagement manager may agree with Marlowe's Group Financial Controller to share specific documents through a temporary encrypted transfer, approved in advance by Globex's Head of IT, provided the files remain stored in the UK.
- Globex's hosting provider, Tessera Cloud, has been asked not to schedule maintenance in its Bahrain region between 15 January and 31 March 2027. Marlowe's files are in the London region in any case.
