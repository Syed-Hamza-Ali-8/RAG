# Salary Bands 2026

## Purpose

This document sets 2026 base salary ranges for Globex Partners LLP staff in the Lahore office. It is confidential to the HR team and partners.

## Bands (Lahore, PKR per month)

| Grade | Title | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Associate | 150,000 | 220,000 |
| G2 | Senior Associate | 250,000 | 360,000 |
| G3 | Assistant Manager | 380,000 | 500,000 |
| G4 | Manager | 550,000 | 750,000 |
| G5 | Senior Manager | 800,000 | 1,050,000 |
| G6 | Director | 1,100,000 | 1,500,000 |

Partners are remunerated through profit share and are not on a salary band.

## Salary Review

Salaries are reviewed once a year in July, after the June performance review.

## Exam Bonus

Staff receive a bonus of PKR 50,000 for each ACCA or ICAP paper passed at the first attempt.

## Confidentiality

Salary information must not be shared outside HR and the partner group.
