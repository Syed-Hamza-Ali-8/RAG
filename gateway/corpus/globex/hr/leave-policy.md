# Leave Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Globex Partners LLP in Lahore, Abu Dhabi and Manchester. Partners follow the partnership agreement. Where local law gives a more generous entitlement, local law applies. The policy is owned by Aisha Farooq, HR Director, and summarised in the Staff Handbook.

## Annual Leave

### Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement. Employees who join or leave during the year receive a pro-rata entitlement for the months worked.

### Carryover

Up to 10 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 30 June, after busy season, or they are forfeited. Days above the carryover limit are not paid out except on leaving.

### Busy Season Restriction

From 15 January to 31 March, annual leave of more than three consecutive days needs approval from the engagement partner. Engagement partners approve such leave only for weddings, family emergencies or leave booked before the employee joined Globex. Leave of up to three days during busy season needs only line manager approval.

### Requesting Leave

Leave is requested through the HR system and approved by the line manager, normally within three working days. Requests for more than five consecutive days should be made at least four weeks in advance. Audit staff should also tell the engagement manager of every engagement they are booked on, so that client fieldwork is not affected.

### Leave After Busy Season

Globex encourages staff to take at least five consecutive days of leave between April and June each year. Staff who worked Saturdays in busy season also take their time off in lieu in this period.

## Sick Leave

### Entitlement

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over.

### Reporting

Tell your line manager and your engagement manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

### Extended Illness

If an illness lasts longer than the paid entitlement, HR will agree next steps, which may include additional paid leave approved by the partners, unpaid leave or a phased return.

## Parental Leave

Maternity leave is 18 weeks at full pay. Paternity leave is 2 weeks at full pay, to be taken within three months of the birth or adoption. Adoptive parents receive the same entitlements as birth parents. Employees returning from maternity leave are not assigned to engagements with busy-season fieldwork in their first three months back unless they ask to be.

## Study Leave

Employees studying for ACCA, ICAEW or ICAP examinations receive 5 days of paid study leave per exam sitting, including resits paid for by the firm. Study leave must be booked at least four weeks before the exam and is normally taken in the two weeks before it. Study leave for exams in March is protected from busy-season restrictions.

## Bereavement and Compassionate Leave

Employees receive 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member and 2 days for other close relatives. Additional compassionate leave may be granted by the HR Director.

## Religious Leave

Employees may take up to 10 working days of leave for Hajj or Umrah once during their employment, after two years of service, in addition to annual leave. Requests should be made at least three months in advance and are not normally approved during busy season.

## Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to 20 days per year may be approved by the HR Director. Health insurance continues during unpaid leave; provident fund contributions pause.

## Public Holidays

Each office observes its country's public holidays. Staff who attend a client inventory count or fieldwork on a public holiday receive a replacement day off, to be taken after busy season.

## Records

All leave is recorded in the HR system. HR reminds staff in October if they have more than 15 unused days, so that they can plan leave before busy season.

## Worked Examples

**A week off in February.** An Assistant Manager wants five days of leave in mid-February for a family wedding. Because the leave is more than three consecutive days in busy season, it needs the engagement partner's approval. Weddings are one of the reasons partners normally approve.

**Carrying over leave.** An employee has 12 unused days at the end of the year. Ten days carry over and must be used by 30 June; two are forfeited.

**Study leave for a resit.** An Associate fails an ACCA paper and resits it in June. The firm pays for one resit per paper, and 5 days of study leave apply to the resit as well. The PKR 50,000 exam bonus applies only to first-attempt passes, so no bonus is paid for passing the resit.

**Sick during busy season.** An employee is ill for four days in March. They tell their line manager and engagement manager before 10:00 on the first day and provide a medical certificate, because the absence is longer than three consecutive days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do Saturdays worked in busy season give extra leave?** Associates and Senior Associates receive one day of time off in lieu for every two Saturdays worked, taken between April and June.

**Can I take leave during the ISO audit in March?** Only staff selected for audit interviews need to be available on the interview day.

**Does leave reset on my work anniversary?** No. Leave runs by calendar year, from 1 January to 31 December.

**Can I take leave on the day of a client inventory count I was assigned to?** Only if the engagement manager finds a replacement; inventory counts on 31 December and early January are planned well in advance.
