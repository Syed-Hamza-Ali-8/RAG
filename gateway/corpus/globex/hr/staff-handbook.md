# Globex Staff Handbook

## 1. About This Handbook

This handbook explains how working at Globex Partners LLP works for employees in Lahore, Abu Dhabi and Manchester. Partners follow the partnership agreement instead, except where this handbook says otherwise. Where local employment law gives you more favourable terms, local law applies. The handbook is owned by Aisha Farooq, HR Director, and reviewed every July after busy season.

## 2. Joining Globex

### 2.1 Day One

On your first day you receive a firm laptop, a Microsoft 365 account with single sign-on through Microsoft Entra ID, access to Ledger Vault (our client document portal) and an introduction to Fernbook, our audit working-paper platform. Each joiner is assigned a counselling partner who supports their development throughout their time at Globex.

### 2.2 Independence Declaration

As an audit firm we must be independent of our audit clients. All new joiners complete an independence declaration on day one, listing financial interests (shares, loans, insurance policies) and close family relationships with any client or client director. You must update your declaration within five working days of any change, and confirm it every year in June.

### 2.3 Probation

Probation lasts six months. Your counselling partner and line manager hold a review at three months and at the end of probation. During probation, either side may end employment with two weeks' notice.

### 2.4 Mandatory Training

In your first month you must complete information security, anti-money laundering and Fernbook fundamentals training. Audit staff must also complete the firm's audit methodology course before their first busy season.

## 3. Working Hours and Busy Season

### 3.1 Normal Hours

Office hours are 9:30 to 17:30 local time, Monday to Friday.

### 3.2 Busy Season

Busy season runs from 15 January to 31 March, when most December year-end audits are completed. During busy season audit teams may work extended hours and Saturdays. The firm provides overtime meals for anyone working after 20:00 or on Saturdays, and transport home after 22:00. Annual leave of more than three consecutive days during busy season needs the engagement partner's approval.

### 3.3 Overtime and Time Off

Associates (G1) and Senior Associates (G2) receive one day of time off in lieu for every two Saturdays worked in busy season, to be taken between April and June.

## 4. Where You Work

### 4.1 Remote Working

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week. During busy season (15 January to 31 March) audit staff work from the office or the client site every day.

### 4.2 Internet Allowance

Lahore staff receive an internet allowance of PKR 6,000 per month. Abu Dhabi and Manchester staff receive a local equivalent.

### 4.3 Working From Abroad

Working from outside your home country requires approval from the HR Director, and from the engagement partner for any client work. Some engagement letters restrict where client files may be accessed; for example, UK client files are stored only in Ledger Vault's London storage, and Lahore staff may access them only through Ledger Vault on a firm-managed laptop.

### 4.4 Security When Working Remotely

Client files must only be accessed through Ledger Vault or Microsoft 365 on a firm-managed laptop. Never download client files to personal devices.

## 5. Pay and Benefits

### 5.1 Salary and Grades

Grades run from G1 (Associate) to G6 (Director). Salary bands are confidential to HR and the partner group. Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month.

### 5.2 Salary Review

Salaries are reviewed once a year in July, after the June performance review.

### 5.3 Performance Bonus

Employees rated A are eligible for a bonus of up to 20% of base salary, and B up to 10%. C and D ratings receive no performance bonus.

### 5.4 Exam Support

Globex supports staff studying for ACCA, ICAEW and ICAP qualifications:

- exam and tuition fees are paid by the firm for first attempts;
- 5 days of paid study leave per exam sitting;
- a bonus of PKR 50,000 for each ACCA or ICAP paper passed at the first attempt (Lahore), or a local equivalent in other offices.

Staff who leave within two years of qualifying repay a share of tuition fees, reducing monthly over the two years.

### 5.5 Health Insurance

Employees and their immediate families are covered by group health insurance from day one. Lahore staff have an annual family inpatient limit of PKR 1.2 million.

### 5.6 Retirement

Lahore employees join the provident fund, with employee and firm each contributing 10% of base salary. Abu Dhabi employees receive end-of-service gratuity as required by UAE law. Manchester employees join a workplace pension with a 5% employer contribution.

## 6. Leave

This section summarises the Leave Policy.

- Annual leave: 25 working days per year; up to 10 unused days carry over and must be used by 30 June.
- Sick leave: 12 days per year; a medical certificate is needed for absences longer than three consecutive days.
- Maternity leave: 18 weeks at full pay. Paternity leave: 2 weeks at full pay.
- Study leave: 5 days per exam sitting.

## 7. Professional Conduct

### 7.1 Ethics and Independence

You must follow the Code of Conduct and the firm's independence rules. Gifts over USD 25 must be declared to the Ethics Partner and are normally declined.

### 7.2 Client Confidentiality

Client information may only be used for the engagement it was provided for. Discuss client matters only with the engagement team.

### 7.3 Continuing Professional Development

Qualified staff must record at least 40 hours of continuing professional development each year, including at least 21 verifiable hours.

## 8. Information Technology

### 8.1 IT Service Desk

Email the IT service desk or call extension 2020. The service desk is staffed from 8:00 to 20:00 during busy season and 9:00 to 18:00 otherwise. The IT team is led by Kwame Mensah, Head of IT.

### 8.2 Multi-Factor Authentication

All staff accounts use Microsoft Entra ID single sign-on with number-matching multi-factor authentication, introduced on 22 August 2026 after the phishing incident of 19 August 2026. Never approve a sign-in request you did not start; report it to the service desk immediately.

### 8.3 Phishing

All staff completed phishing awareness training by 30 September 2026. Report suspicious emails using the "Report phishing" button in Outlook. Suspected mailbox compromises must be reported to the Legal Counsel within one hour.

### 8.4 Ledger Vault and Fernbook

Client files are exchanged only through Ledger Vault, which is hosted on Tessera Cloud. Audit working papers are prepared in Fernbook and link to evidence in Ledger Vault. Do not store client files on your laptop's local disk.

## 9. Performance and Development

Globex holds one annual performance review in June, after busy season. Engagement managers give feedback for every engagement you work on. Ratings are A, B, C or D and are moderated by the partners' committee, chaired by the Managing Partner, Imran Siddiqui. Employees rated D receive a 90-day development plan agreed with their counselling partner.

## 10. Expenses

Expenses are claimed in Claimly, the firm's expense app, within 45 days with receipts. The Expense Policy sets approval limits and per diem rates.

## 11. Grievances and Discipline

### 11.1 Grievances

Raise concerns first with your line manager or counselling partner. If that does not resolve them, raise a formal grievance in writing with the HR Director, who will meet you within ten working days.

### 11.2 Disciplinary Procedure

Globex follows a fair procedure with an investigation, a hearing, a written decision and a right of appeal. Breaches of independence rules or client confidentiality may be reported to professional bodies as well as leading to disciplinary action.

### 11.3 Speaking Up

Concerns about wrongdoing can be raised with the Legal Counsel, Henrik Dahl, or the Ethics Partner. Retaliation is prohibited.

## 12. Leaving Globex

### 12.1 Notice Periods

After probation, notice periods are one month for G1 and G2, two months for G3 and G4, and three months for G5 and G6.

### 12.2 Leaving Process

Before your last day, hand over your engagements, update the independence system, and return your laptop to IT. Access to Ledger Vault and Fernbook is removed at the end of your last day. Unused annual leave is paid in your final salary.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I take a week off in February?

Only with your engagement partner's approval, because February is busy season. Leave of up to three days does not need partner approval.

### What happens if I fail an exam paper?

Globex pays for one resit per paper. Study leave applies to the resit as well. The exam bonus is paid only for first-attempt passes.

### Who is my counselling partner?

Your counselling partner is named in your offer letter and shown in the HR system. You may ask HR to change counselling partner once a year.

## 14. Health, Safety and Wellbeing

### 14.1 Health and Safety

Each office has a trained first aider and fire warden. Report accidents and near misses to the office manager on the same day. Employees who work remotely should complete the home workstation checklist in the HR system.

### 14.2 Wellbeing Support

All employees can use a confidential counselling and advice service. During busy season, HR runs weekly wellbeing check-ins and engagement partners must review team hours every Friday. Anyone working more than 60 hours a week for three consecutive weeks should raise it with their counselling partner.

## 15. Equal Opportunities

Globex recruits, promotes and pays on merit and does not tolerate discrimination, harassment or bullying. Complaints are handled through the grievance procedure or the speaking-up route. The HR Director reports gender and grade representation to the partners every year.

## 16. Personal Data About Employees

Globex holds employee personal data for payroll, benefits, independence monitoring and regulatory reporting. Access is limited to HR, payroll and partners who need it. Independence declarations are also reviewed by the Ethics Partner. You may request a copy of your data from HR.

## 17. Use of Firm Systems

Firm systems, including email, Teams, Ledger Vault and Fernbook, may be monitored for security and compliance purposes. Limited personal use of email is allowed, but never use firm systems for personal business or to store personal files. Installing unapproved software on firm laptops is not allowed; request software through the IT service desk.

## 18. Social Media and Public Statements

You may say that you work at Globex, but do not identify clients or discuss client work online. Only partners authorised by the Managing Partner may speak to the media for the firm.

## 19. Outside Interests

Outside employment, directorships or investments must be approved by HR and the Ethics Partner before you take them on, because they may affect the firm's independence from audit clients.

## 20. References

Only HR gives employment references on behalf of Globex. References confirm dates of employment, grade and job title.

## 21. Training and Career Development

### 21.1 Career Path

The typical audit career path at Globex is Associate (G1), Senior Associate (G2), Assistant Manager (G3), Manager (G4), Senior Manager (G5) and Director (G6), with admission to partnership decided by the partners each year. Promotions are decided in the July salary review, based on the June performance review, qualifications and readiness for the next role.

### 21.2 Training Plan

Every employee agrees a training plan with their counselling partner each July. The plan covers technical training (auditing and accounting standards updates, tax law changes), Fernbook and data analytics skills, and professional skills such as report writing and client communication.

### 21.3 Professional Memberships

Globex pays the annual membership fees for one professional body (ACCA, ICAEW or ICAP) for all qualified staff.

### 21.4 Secondments

Staff at G3 and above may apply for secondments of three to six months between the Lahore, Abu Dhabi and Manchester offices. Secondees keep their home grade and salary and receive a housing and travel allowance. Secondments are planned outside busy season where possible.

## 22. Travel

Business travel is approved by the engagement partner and booked through the finance team. Economy class is used for all flights under eight hours. Per diems and approval limits are set in the Expense Policy; for example, the per diem in the UAE is USD 110 per day.

## 23. Year-End Inventory Counts

Audit staff may be asked to attend client inventory counts on 31 December or in the first days of January, for example at sampled stores of Marlowe Retail Group. Staff who attend a count on a public holiday receive a replacement day off, to be taken after busy season.

## 24. Changes to This Handbook

HR announces changes to this handbook by email at least 30 days before they take effect, unless a change is required by law sooner.

## 26. Acknowledgement

All employees confirm in the HR system, within their first week and again after any major update, that they have read this handbook and the Code of Conduct and understand their independence obligations. Partners confirm the same through the partnership's annual compliance declaration in June.

## 27. Busy Season Guide

### 27.1 Before Busy Season

In December, each audit employee confirms their engagement schedule for January to March with their engagement managers, books any study leave for March exams, and checks that their laptop, Fernbook access and Ledger Vault access work. The IT team runs a Ledger Vault capacity review in December, after the March 2026 outage showed that uploads can triple at the end of busy season.

### 27.2 During Busy Season

Audit teams work from the office or client sites every day. Engagement partners review team hours every Friday. Overtime meals are provided after 20:00 and on Saturdays, and transport home is arranged after 22:00. Annual leave longer than three consecutive days needs the engagement partner's approval.

### 27.3 If Ledger Vault Is Unavailable

Tell your engagement manager immediately and continue with work that does not need new evidence, such as reviewing working papers in Fernbook. Do not ask clients to email documents instead. The engagement manager will agree any temporary arrangement with the Head of IT, keeping UK client files in the UK.

### 27.4 After Busy Season

Time off in lieu for Saturdays worked is taken between April and June. HR encourages everyone to take at least five consecutive days of leave in this period. The annual performance review takes place in June.

## 28. Independence in Practice

### 28.1 What You May Not Do

You may not hold shares, bonds or other financial interests in any audit client, including through a pension or investment fund you control; take loans from an audit client, other than from a bank on normal terms; or accept gifts or hospitality above USD 25 from a client.

### 28.2 Family Relationships

If a close family member works for an audit client in a financial reporting role, tell the Ethics Partner before you are assigned to that client.

### 28.3 Checking a Company

Before buying shares, search the firm's restricted entity list in the independence system. If a company is on the list, you may not invest in it.

### 28.4 Breaches

If you discover that you have breached an independence rule, for example by inheriting shares in an audit client, tell the Ethics Partner within five working days. Most breaches can be resolved by selling the interest and documenting the steps taken; hiding a breach is a disciplinary matter.

## 29. Data Protection for Staff

All staff handle personal data in client files, such as payroll extracts in tax work for clients like Sunridge Schools Trust. Follow the restrictions agreed in each engagement: keep personal data in Ledger Vault's restricted folders, share it only with the named team members, and delete extracts when the engagement says so. Report any suspected data breach to the IT service desk and to Henrik Dahl, Legal Counsel, immediately; Globex's engagement letters commit us to notify affected clients within 72 hours.

## 31. Worked Examples

### 31.1 Study Leave Before a March Exam

An Associate is sitting an ACCA paper on 10 March, in busy season. Study leave of 5 days per sitting is protected from busy-season restrictions, so the Associate books it at least four weeks ahead, normally for the two weeks before the exam, and tells the engagement manager. If they pass at the first attempt, they receive the PKR 50,000 exam bonus.

### 31.2 Carrying Over Leave

An employee has 14 unused days at the end of the year. Up to 10 days carry over and must be used by 30 June; the remaining 4 days are forfeited. HR's October reminder is meant to prevent this.

### 31.3 A Relative Joins an Audit Client

An employee's sister is appointed financial controller at Marlowe Retail Group, an audit client. The employee tells the Ethics Partner and updates their independence declaration within five working days. The employee will not be assigned to the Marlowe audit.

### 31.4 A Suspicious Sign-In Prompt

An employee receives a Microsoft sign-in approval request on their phone while not signing in. Since 22 August 2026 prompts use number matching, so there is no number to enter. The employee denies the request and reports it to the IT service desk on extension 2020. If a mailbox compromise is suspected, IT informs the Legal Counsel within one hour.

### 31.5 Working From Abu Dhabi on a UK Engagement

A Senior Associate from Lahore is visiting the Abu Dhabi office and wants to work on Marlowe's audit. Marlowe's files stay in Ledger Vault's London storage and may be accessed through Ledger Vault on a firm-managed laptop. Working outside the home country needs HR Director approval, and client work needs the engagement partner's approval too.
